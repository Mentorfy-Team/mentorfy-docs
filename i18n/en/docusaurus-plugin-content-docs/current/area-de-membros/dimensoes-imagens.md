# Image dimensions

Reference guide for image uploads — customization, SEO, and content.

This document lists all dimensions, aspect ratios, and accepted formats for configurable images on the Mentorfy platform: brand identity, login area, home page, SEO, categories, and mentorship covers. Use it as a quick reference when preparing artwork for upload.

## 1. Brand identity

| Element | Dimension / ratio | Accepted formats | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Logo** | 1:1 scale — max 2000×2000 px | PNG, JPG, WebP | Displayed in the student area. AI generation supported. |

## 2. Login area

*Applies when the selected layout includes a storefront next to the login form.*

| Element | Dimension / ratio | Accepted formats | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Custom carousel (storefront)** | Default: 900×1440 px | PNG, JPG, WebP | Up to 4 images total, combining existing product covers + custom images. Independent from the hero banner slider. |
| **Login background image** | Fixed 16:9 ratio | PNG, JPG, WebP | Crop/focal point cannot be adjusted, unlike the Hero Banner. |

## 3. Home

| Element | Dimension / ratio | Accepted formats | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Hero Banner (slider)** | Default: 16:9 ratio | PNG, JPG, WebP | Presentation can be adjusted (see details below). |
| **Home background image** | Fixed 16:9 ratio | PNG, JPG, WebP | Main page background. No crop adjustment option. |

### Hero Banner presentation settings

The banner is cropped to fill the screen. You can choose the aspect ratio and focal point that should survive the crop, independently for desktop and mobile:

| Element | Dimension / ratio | Accepted formats | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Desktop** | 21:9, 16:9 (default), 16:10, 3:1 | PNG, JPG, WebP | Focal point adjustable on a 3×3 grid. |
| **Mobile** | 16:10 (default), 4:3, 1:1, 4:5 | PNG, JPG, WebP | Focal point adjustable on a 3×3 grid. |

Image fit: **Fill** or **Contain** options.

## 4. SEO

| Element | Dimension / ratio | Accepted formats | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Favicon** | 32×32 px | ICO, PNG, SVG | Icon displayed in the browser tab. |
| **Open Graph (og:image)** | Recommended: 1200×630 px | PNG, JPG | How the page appears when shared on WhatsApp, Facebook, etc. |

## 5. Categories

| Element | Dimension / ratio | Accepted formats | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Category cover (vertical)** | 900×1440 px | PNG, JPG, WebP | — |

## 6. Mentorship covers

| Element | Dimension / ratio | Accepted formats | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Vertical cover** | 900×1440 px | PNG, JPG, WebP | Follow the dimension guidelines to avoid cropping in the final view. |
| **Horizontal cover** | 2160×900 px | PNG, JPG, WebP | Follow the dimension guidelines to avoid cropping in the final view. |
