**Groups organize the people who already bought. Your team is the people who work with you.**

- **Groups:** put students together in a class, or a company with its employees, and track each group on a board, stage by stage.
- **My team:** invite support staff, teachers, and salespeople, and decide what each person can see and change.
- **My Meetings:** schedule live sessions. The recording gets ready on its own for students to watch again.
