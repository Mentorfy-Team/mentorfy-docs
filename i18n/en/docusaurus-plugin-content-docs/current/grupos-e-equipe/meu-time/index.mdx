**Don't do it all alone: your team is the people who work with you, and each person sees only what you allow.**

In the menu, it's under **Teams & Groups** › **My team**.

- **Team:** a set of people who do the same thing, like "Support" or "Teachers".
- **Access:** what each person can do in each area of the dashboard: **None**, **View**, or **Edit**.
- **Team products:** what the people on that team see.
- **Students:** which students each person follows.
