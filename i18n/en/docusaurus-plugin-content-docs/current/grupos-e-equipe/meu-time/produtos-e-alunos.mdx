In this part, you choose which products the team sees and which students each person follows. With no products, people on the team log in and find everything empty.

<Aula slug="times-p3-distribuir" />

## Step by step

<Passos>
  <Passo titulo={<>On the team's card, in <strong>Products the team sees</strong>, click <strong>Manage</strong>.</>} />
  <Passo titulo={<>Choose how to give access: <strong>Every product, including the next ones</strong>, or check whole showcase categories.</>}>
    In **Pick the products**, checking a category takes everything in it. You can also check product by product.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Or, in <strong>Follow groups</strong>, pick a group.</>}>
    The team gets the group's products now and the ones that join it later.

    ![Team products window with Every product, Follow groups, and the list of categories](/img/guia/times-p3-distribuir/02-grupos.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Click <strong>Save products</strong>.</>}>
    The team's card shows which group it follows.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Now, open the person and go to the <strong>Students</strong> tab. Choose <strong>All students</strong> or <strong>Only the students I pick</strong>.</>}>
    ![The person's Students tab with the None, All students, and Only the students I pick options](/img/guia/times-p3-distribuir/06-escolher.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Check that person's students and click <strong>Save</strong>.</>}>
    Done: they follow only those students.
  </Passo>
</Passos>

## Good to know

:::tip
Who runs the whole operation? Use **Every product, including the next ones**. Each new product joins the team on its own, and you never have to remember to give access to a new launch again.
:::

:::caution
Checking a category takes the products that are in it **today**. Products added to the category later don't go along on their own. When that happens, the notice at the top of the page shows "new product missing", with the **Add** button.
:::

## Reference

| Option in the **Students** tab | What the person follows |
| --- | --- |
| **None** | Nobody. They don't show up as the person in charge of any student |
| **All students** | The students of all the team's products, including the ones who join later |
| **Only the students I pick** | Only the students you check |

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>How do I let a support person see only their own students?</summary>

In the person's **Students** tab, choose **Only the students I pick**, check their students, and click **Save**. In the student list, they now see the students you checked. To find them fast, use **Search student** or **Filter by product**, and then **Select all** (with the number found).

</details>

<details>
<summary>The Students tab is empty. Why?</summary>

Students come from the team's products. If the team doesn't see any product yet, give it products first in **Manage**.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I change the products for just one person?</summary>

Not directly. Products come from teams: the person's **Products** tab shows what comes from each team. To change it, manage the team's products, or put the person on another team in the **Teams and more** tab.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I unchecked some students. What happens to them?</summary>

Before you save, the screen shows how many are leaving. When you save, that person stops following them. The students don't lose access to their products because of this.

</details>
