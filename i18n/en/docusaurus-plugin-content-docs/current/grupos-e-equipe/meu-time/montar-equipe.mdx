In **My team**, you build your team and decide what each person can access. Start by creating a team, and then invite people into it.

<Aula slug="meus-times" />

## Step by step

<Passos>
  <Passo titulo={<>In the menu, open <strong>My team</strong> and click <strong>New team</strong>.</>} />
  <Passo titulo={<>Give it a name that says what these people do and click <strong>Create team</strong>.</>}>
    You can tap a suggestion: **Support**, **Teachers**, **Sales**, or **Marketing**. The new team shows up in the list, still without people.

    ![New team window with the name suggestions](/img/guia/meus-times/02-novo.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Inside the team, click <strong>Invite person</strong>.</>} />
  <Passo titulo={<>In <strong>Who is it?</strong>, fill in the <strong>Name</strong> and the <strong>Email</strong>.</>}>
    The team is already checked in **Which team?**. You can check more than one.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>In <strong>What can they access?</strong>, pick a profile, like <strong>Support</strong>.</>}>
    Later, you can fine-tune it area by area in the person's profile.

    ![Invite person window with name, email, team, and access profile](/img/guia/meus-times/07-perfil.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Click <strong>Invite</strong>.</>}>
    Done: the person joins the team and gets the invitation by email. The notice at the top of the page shows what's still missing, like a team with no product.
  </Passo>
</Passos>

## Good to know

:::tip
The **First access password** is optional. If left blank, Mentorfy creates one and sends it by email. If the **Invite** button doesn't unlock, read the **Missing:** line at the bottom: it tells you what still needs to be filled in.
:::

:::caution
A team with no products sees nothing: people log in and find everything empty. Give them products right after inviting. See [Team products and students](/docs/grupos-e-equipe/meu-time/produtos-e-alunos).
:::

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>How many teams can I have? How do I get more?</summary>

It depends on your plan. When you reach the limit, the page shows "You're using X of the Y teams in your plan" and the **New team** button is locked. To create another one, delete an empty team or change plans in **See plans**.

</details>

<details>
<summary>The person already has a Mentorfy account. What now?</summary>

You can invite them as usual, with the same email. They log in with the same login, and their password doesn't change.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I remove someone from the team? What do they lose?</summary>

Open the person and click **Remove from team**. If they're on more than one team, choose which one. Before you confirm, the screen shows what changes:

- they stop seeing the products that came only from that team;
- the students they followed on that team are left without anyone in charge;
- if that was their only team, they lose access to your dashboard. If they're on other teams, they stay on them with the same access.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What if I delete a whole team?</summary>

In the team menu, click **Delete team**. The people on that team stop seeing the products that came from it and stop following the students there. Their access to the dashboard areas stays the same.

</details>
