**A mentorship is what you sell; a group is how you organize the people who bought it.**

- **Board:** the Groups screen. It shows your whole process, from arrival to completion.
- **Column:** a stage of the process, like "Onboarding" or "In progress".
- **Card:** each group is a card. You drag the card from one column to the next as it moves forward.

A group can be a class of students or a company with its employees. It brings together **clients** and **products**. In the menu, it's under **Teams & Groups** › **Groups**.

What you can do here:

- Create groups and pick the products and clients for each one.
- Set up the stages of your process, with a color and a deadline on each column.
- Say who on your team looks after each group and who can see it.
- Have a board for each process or for each person on your team.
- Link calendar meetings to the group, so they count as contact in **Indicators**.
