Calendar meetings with the client count as contact, along with comments. When you link the meeting to the group, **Indicators** shows who is on track and who has gone without contact.

<Aula slug="grupos-p5-reunioes" />

## Step by step

<Passos>
  <Passo titulo={<>On the board, click the group to open it.</>} />
  <Passo titulo={<>In <strong>Meetings with the client</strong>, check the suggestion. If it was with this client, click <strong>Yes, it was</strong>.</>}>
    Mentorfy suggests calendar meetings by their name. If it wasn't, click **No** and it won't be suggested again.

    ![Open group with the Meetings with the client section and the Yes, it was suggestion](/img/guia/grupos-p5-reunioes/01-grupo.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Or click <strong>Link a meeting</strong> and pick the meeting from the list.</>}>
    The list shows **Meetings from the last month**. You can search by name.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Done: the meeting is linked.</>}>
    It counts as contact as soon as it happens. Before that, it shows as **scheduled**.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>After it happens, follow it in <strong>Indicators</strong>, under <strong>Follow-up records</strong>.</>} />
</Passos>

## Good to know

:::tip
If a student in the group was invited to the meeting, it counts on its own: it shows with the **a student of the group was invited** tag, without you needing to link it.
:::

:::info
The **Indicators** button shows up for the account owner and for people with **Edit** on **Groups**.
:::

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>I linked the wrong meeting. How do I undo it?</summary>

In the **Meetings with the client** list, click **Unlink** next to the meeting. It stops counting as contact for that group.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I invited the whole group to a live class. Does that count as contact?</summary>

No. An invitation sent to the whole group is a general notice, not a conversation with the client. For it to count, link the meeting to the group with **Link a meeting**, or invite the student directly.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What else counts as contact?</summary>

Comments on the card and calendar meetings with the client. See the details and how to choose your contact rhythm in [What counts as contact](/docs/alunos/acompanhar/o-que-conta-como-contato).

</details>
