A group brings clients and products together in a single card. Use it to separate classes ("October class") or companies ("Alfa Company · Sales Team") and follow each one from the inside.

On the Mentorfy screen, a student shows up as a **client**, in the **Clients** menu.

<Aula slug="grupos" />

## Step by step

<Passos>
  <Passo titulo={<>In <strong>Groups</strong>, click <strong>New group</strong>, in the top corner.</>}>
    ![Groups board with the New group button in the top corner](/img/guia/grupos/01-quadro.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>In <strong>Information</strong>, fill in the <strong>Name</strong> and the <strong>Description</strong>.</>}>
    The **Expiration date** is optional.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>In <strong>Products</strong>, check the category or product the group will follow.</>}>
    Use **By category** to check a whole showcase category, or **By product** to pick them one by one.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>In <strong>Clients</strong>, check who joins the group.</>}>
    Click **All** to check the whole list, or search by name. The list only shows up after you pick at least one product.

    ![Picking the products and clients for the new group](/img/guia/grupos/05-clientes.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Click <strong>Create and add clients</strong>.</>}>
    Done: the group already shows up on the board. With no clients checked, the button is called **Create group**.
  </Passo>
</Passos>

## Good to know

:::caution
Each student is in only one group per product. If you check someone who's already in another group, the screen warns you first and lets you choose: **Add only the one without a group**, **Create group without moving anyone**, or move them anyway. Anyone who is moved leaves their old group.
:::

:::tip
To change products, clients, or access expiration later, open the group and click **Add products or clients**.
:::

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>Do the group's students get access to the product on their own?</summary>

Only if you turn that on. Open the group, click **Add products or clients**, and in the products, turn on **Automatically grant access**. When you save, every client in the group gets access to the checked products, and anyone who joins the group later gets it too.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I can't find a client in the list. Why?</summary>

The client list shows people who have the products you checked for the group. Check that the client has access to one of those products.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I delete a group? What happens to the students?</summary>

Open the group, click **Options** and then **Remove card**. The group is deleted for good, and the students keep their access to the products. If there are students inside, they're left without a group and don't go back on their own: that's why the screen asks you to confirm you understand before **Delete group**.

</details>
