Schedule your live sessions in **My Meetings**. With recording turned on, the video gets ready on its own after the session.

<Aula slug="reunioes" />

## Step by step

<Passos>
  <Passo titulo={<>In <strong>My Meetings</strong>, click <strong>New Meeting</strong>.</>} />
  <Passo titulo={<>Choose <strong>Mentorfy Meeting</strong>, give it a name, and set the date and time. Click <strong>Next</strong>.</>}>
    ![Basic step with Mentorfy Meeting, name, date, and time](/img/guia/reunioes/03-basico.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>In the invitation, choose groups, students, or emails. If you prefer, click <strong>Skip</strong> and send the link later.</>}>
    Use the **Groups**, **Students**, **Email**, or **List** tabs. In **How to notify**, choose **Google Calendar**, **Email**, or **WhatsApp**.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>In the settings, leave <strong>Record Meeting</strong> turned on. With <strong>Auto Record</strong>, recording starts on its own.</>}>
    ![Settings step with Meeting Type and the Record Meeting and Auto Record switches](/img/guia/reunioes/06-gravacao.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Click <strong>Create meeting</strong>.</>} />
  <Passo titulo={<>Done! Copy the <strong>Guest link</strong> and send it to your students.</>}>
    To join yourself, use the **Moderator link (you)** or the **Enter meeting** button.
  </Passo>
</Passos>

## Good to know

:::tip
Invited a group? Anyone who joins the group later also gets the invitation until the meeting ends, and can watch the recording.
:::

:::info
**Mentorfy Meeting** is a subscription service, purchased separately. When you subscribe to the platform, you may get 30 days of Meetings free. Without it, connect Zoom or Google Meet in **Integrations** and create the meeting there.
:::

## Reference

| Meeting Type | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Basic Meeting** | Quick, casual conversations |
| **Corporate** | Professional, with more control and security |
| **Webinar** | Presentations and large events |
| **Custom** | Settings your way |

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>Mentorfy Meeting is locked. Why?</summary>

It requires a separate subscription. If your Zoom or Google Meet is connected in **Integrations**, create the meeting with them. To subscribe, use the plans button that shows in **My Meetings**.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Does Google Meet record?</summary>

It does if you use a Google Workspace account with recording allowed. If it isn't allowed, the room is created without automatic recording.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I invited too many people. What happens?</summary>

The Mentorfy room has a participant limit, shown in the invitation step. If you go over it, the screen warns you: invite fewer people or use Zoom or Google Meet.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I delete a meeting?</summary>

Yes, in the meeting menu, with **Delete meeting**. But the links and invitations for that room stop working, and it can't be undone.

</details>
