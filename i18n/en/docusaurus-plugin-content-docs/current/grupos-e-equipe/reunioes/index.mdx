**Schedule your live sessions here. The recording gets ready on its own, for students to watch again.**

In the menu, it's under **Meetings** › **My Meetings**.

- Create meetings in the Mentorfy room (**Mentorfy Meeting**), on **Zoom**, or on **Google Meet**.
- Invite groups, students, or any email, and notify them by Google Calendar, email, or WhatsApp.
- Watch the recording and copy the link to send to your students.

:::info
**Mentorfy Meeting** is a subscription service, purchased separately: it isn't included in any platform plan. Prefer to use your own Zoom or Google Meet account? Connect it in **Integrations** and create your meetings there.
:::
