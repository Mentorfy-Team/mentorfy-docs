**Build once and reuse: your questions are saved in the Question Bank, ready to become a quiz in any mentorship.**

- **Create categories** by topic, like "Leadership".
- **Write the questions** with the options, the right answer, and an explanation.
- **Use them in a lesson**: the multiple-choice step pulls the questions from the category.
