The Bank's questions become a quiz inside the lesson, without typing everything again. You choose the category and how many questions the student will answer.

<Aula slug="banco-p2-aula" />

## Step by step

<Passos>
  <Passo titulo={<>In the mentorship editor, click <strong>Add step</strong> and go to <strong>Activities and Assessments</strong>.</>} />
  <Passo titulo={<>Choose <strong>Perguntas de Múltipla Escolha</strong> (Multiple Choice Questions) and click <strong>Add to Course</strong>.</>} />
  <Passo titulo={<>Click <strong>Edit</strong> on the step and give it a title.</>} />
  <Passo titulo={<>In <strong>Question Category</strong>, choose the category from the bank.</>} />
  <Passo titulo={<>In <strong>Number of Questions</strong>, say how many the student will answer and click <strong>Save</strong>.</>}>
    Below the field you see the maximum, which is the total number of questions in the category.

    ![Multiple Choice window with the Math category and 5 questions, maximum 13](/img/guia/banco-p2-aula/08-quantidade.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Click <strong>Save changes</strong>, at the top of the editor.</>}>
    Done: the quiz is now in the lesson.
  </Passo>
</Passos>

## Good to know

:::tip Want questions just for this lesson?
In the same step, use **Create questions manually** to write questions that don't go into the bank.
:::

:::info A quiz can unlock the next module
A module can be unlocked by the score in the Multiple Choice steps of the previous module. If the previous module has no quiz, the next one is unlocked automatically.
:::

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>I got "Invalid question count". What do I do?</summary>

The number must be at least 1 and at most the total number of questions in the category. Lower the number or create more questions in the Bank.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What does the student see after answering?</summary>

After answering, the student sees the explanation you wrote in the question. That's why it's worth putting care into it. See [Save your questions](/docs/cursos/banco-de-questoes/guardar-perguntas).

</details>
