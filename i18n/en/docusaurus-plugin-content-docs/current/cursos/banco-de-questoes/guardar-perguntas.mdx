Questions are saved in the **Question Bank** and become a quiz in any mentorship. Create the category first: without a category, the question isn't created.

<Aula slug="banco-questoes" />

## Step by step

<Passos>
  <Passo titulo={<>In the menu, open <strong>Question Bank</strong>. On the <strong>Categories</strong> tab, click <strong>New Category</strong>.</>}>
    Give it a name, like "Leadership", and save.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>On the <strong>Questions</strong> tab, click <strong>New Question</strong> and write the question in <strong>Question Title</strong>.</>} />
  <Passo titulo={<>In <strong>Categories</strong>, choose the category. Then choose the <strong>Difficulty Level</strong>.</>}>
    Difficulty goes from **Very Easy** to **Very Hard**.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>In <strong>Answer Options</strong>, write the options and mark the correct one. Then click <strong>Next Step</strong>.</>}>
    You need at least 2 options. Use **Add Option** to add more.

    ![Question form with the category, difficulty, and options, with the correct one marked](/img/guia/banco-questoes/09-opcoes.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Write the explanation and click <strong>Save</strong>.</>}>
    The student sees the explanation after answering. Done: the question is now in the bank.
  </Passo>
</Passos>

## Good to know

:::tip The explanation can be text, a file, or audio
You need to use at least one of the three. The file can be an image or a PDF, and you can record the audio on the spot.
:::

:::info Subcategories help separate topics
On the **Subcategories** tab, you split a category into smaller parts. To find a question later, use **Filter by category**.
:::

## Reference

| Field | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| **Question Title** | at least 3 characters |
| **Categories** | at least one |
| **Difficulty Level** | from 1 (Very Easy) to 5 (Very Hard) |
| **Answer Options** | at least 2, with one marked as correct |
| **Question Explanation** | text, file, or audio (at least one) |

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>I got "Select at least one category". What do I do?</summary>

The question needs a category. If you don't have one yet, go to the **Categories** tab, click **New Category**, and then go back to the question.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I can't get past the first step of the question.</summary>

Check that: the question has at least 3 characters, there are at least 2 options filled in, and one of them is marked as correct. The screen shows what's missing.

</details>
