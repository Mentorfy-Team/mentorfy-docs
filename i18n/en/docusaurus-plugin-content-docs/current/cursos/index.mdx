**A mentorship (or product) is what you sell: a course with modules and lessons. People who buy it get access and find everything in your showcase, the home page of the student area.**

Here you learn how to:

- create the mentorship and build the modules and lessons;
- upload videos to **Storage**;
- give out automatic certificates and build quizzes with the **Question Bank**;
- organize the showcase and use your own web address.
