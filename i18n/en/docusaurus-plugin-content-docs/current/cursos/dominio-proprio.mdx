With your own domain, the showcase and the student area live at your address, with your brand. A domain is your website's address, like myschool.com. The platform guides you step by step.

<Aula slug="dominio" />

## Step by step

<Passos>
  <Passo titulo={<>In the menu, open <strong>Custom Domain</strong> and click <strong>Connect my domain</strong>.</>} />
  <Passo titulo={<>Say whether you already have a domain.</>}>
    Choose **Yes, I have one**. If you don't have one yet, **Not yet** registers a new one without leaving the platform.

    ![The question Do you already have a domain? with the options Yes, I have one and Not yet](/img/guia/dominio/02-conectar.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Type your domain and click <strong>Analyze</strong>.</>}>
    The platform finds out where it is set up and whether there is already a website or email working on it.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo="Choose how to connect.">
    **Automatic connection (1 change only)** or **Guided connection (a few lines)**. See the difference in Reference.

    ![Analysis result with the options Automatic connection and Guided connection](/img/guia/dominio/05-analise.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo="Make the change in the panel where you bought the domain.">
    The screen shows what to copy and paste, with the step by step for your provider. The platform checks by itself every 30 seconds.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo="Done: your domain is live.">
    Website and security certificate working. If you want, click **Turn on email on my domain** so students get emails from your address.
  </Passo>
</Passos>

## Good to know

:::info It may take a while
The change can take from a few minutes up to 24 hours to work across the whole internet (usually less than 1 hour). You can close the screen: the platform keeps checking and lets you know by email when it's live.
:::

:::caution If there is already a website on this domain
Once connected, the address starts showing your showcase. The analysis warns you when it finds a website or email in use.
:::

## Reference

| How to connect | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| **Automatic connection (1 change only)** | You change a single setting (the "DNS servers") on the site where you bought the domain. Website, certificate, and email set themselves up. Control of the domain moves to Mentorfy |
| **Guided connection (a few lines)** | You create a few lines in your domain's panel, following the step by step. Nothing that already exists is changed |

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>I use Cloudflare. Is there anything to watch out for?</summary>

Yes. On the website lines, keep the orange cloud turned off (gray, "DNS only"). With it on, the security certificate doesn't work.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I want a .com.br domain. Can I register it here?</summary>

Not yet. Register it at registro.br (it takes about 10 minutes), then come back and choose **Yes, I have one**.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I got "This domain is already connected to another mentor's account".</summary>

The domain is linked to another Mentorfy account. If it's yours, contact support: [Support](/docs/conta/suporte).

</details>

<details>
<summary>What happens if I disconnect the domain?</summary>

The showcase goes back to using Mentorfy's default address. You can connect it again whenever you want.

</details>
