**People who finish your mentorship get a certificate with your brand, without you having to do anything.**

- **Prepare the certificate** for each mentorship in four steps: Design, Texts, Signature, and When to issue.
- **Set up your brand once** (logo, color, and signature) and see who has already received a certificate, issuing it by hand when you need to.
- **Use your own artwork** and, if you want, a **default certificate** for mentorships that don't have their own.
