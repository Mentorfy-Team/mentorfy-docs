**People who finish your mentorship get a certificate with your brand, without you having to do anything.**

- **Prepare the certificate** for each mentorship in four steps: Design, Texts, Signature, and When to issue.
- **Set up your brand once** (logo, color, and signature) and use it on all of them.
- **See who has already received one** and issue it manually when you need to.
