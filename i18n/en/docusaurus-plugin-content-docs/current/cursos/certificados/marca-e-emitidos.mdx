In **Brand and signature**, you set your logo, color, and signature once, and they apply to all certificates. In **Issued**, you see who has already received one and can issue it right away to a student.

<Aula slug="certificados-p2-marca" />

## Step by step

<Passos>
  <Passo titulo={<>On the <strong>Certificates</strong> screen, click <strong>Brand and signature</strong>.</>}>
    The button is in the top right corner.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Fill in your brand and click <strong>Save brand</strong>.</>}>
    Add the **Brand name**, the **Logo**, the **Accent color**, and who signs.

    ![Brand and signature window with name, logo, color, and default signature](/img/guia/certificados-p2-marca/02-marca.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>To see who has already received one, click <strong>Issued</strong> on the mentorship.</>} />
  <Passo titulo={<>Need to give it out sooner? Click <strong>Issue to a student</strong>.</>}>
    Search for the student by name or email and click **Issue**. It works even if they haven't met the rule.

    ![Issue to a student window with the search and the list of students](/img/guia/certificados-p2-marca/06-emitir.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo="Done: the certificate shows up in the list.">
    The list shows the **Code** and the **Status**, for example whether the student has already downloaded it or hasn't opened it yet.
  </Passo>
</Passos>

## Good to know

:::tip You can change it for each mentorship
The brand applies to all certificates, but you can change the logo, color, and signature on a specific mentorship's certificate.
:::

:::info Asked for the student's ID or signature?
If the certificate asks for the student's ID (CPF) or signature, it only goes out after the student provides it. In the list, the status shows as **Waiting for ID** or **Waiting for signature**.
:::

## Reference

| File | Accepted format |
| --- | --- |
| Logo | PNG with a transparent background, or PDF. Image up to 2 MB, PDF up to 10 MB |
| Signature image | PNG with a transparent background, or PDF. Image up to 2 MB, PDF up to 10 MB |

No signature image? Turn on **Use handwritten lettering instead of an image**: the platform writes the name in a signature-style font.

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>The student says they didn't get the email. How do I resend it?</summary>

In **Issued**, open the actions for the student's certificate and use **Resend email**. You can also **Download PDF** and send it to them.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I issued it by mistake. Can I cancel it?</summary>

Yes. In the certificate's actions, use **Revoke**. The validation page starts showing "invalid" and the student loses the download. If you change your mind, use **Reactivate**.

</details>
