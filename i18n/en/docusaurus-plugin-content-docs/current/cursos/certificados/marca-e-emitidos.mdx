In **Brand and signature**, you set the name, logo, color, and signer once, and it applies to all certificates. In **Issued**, you see who already got a certificate and take care of each one.

<Aula slug="certificados-p2-marca" />

## Step by step

<Passos>
  <Passo titulo={<>On the <strong>Certificates</strong> screen, click <strong>Brand and signature</strong>.</>}>
    The button is in the top right corner.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Fill in your brand and click <strong>Save brand</strong>.</>}>
    Enter the **Brand name**, the **Logo**, the **Accent color**, and, under **Default signature**, the name and role of the person who signs.

    ![Brand and signature window with name, logo, color, and default signature](/img/guia/certificados-p2-marca/02-marca.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>To see who already got one, click <strong>Issued</strong> on the mentorship.</>}>
    At the top, you see how many certificates went out, how many students downloaded, and how many need attention. The bar below shows the rule that's active, with the **Change rule** link.

    ![Issued screen with the counters, the filters, and the list of students](/img/guia/certificados-p2-marca/03-emitidos.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Need to deliver early? Click <strong>Issue to a student</strong>.</>}>
    Search for the student by name or email and click **Issue**. It works even if they haven't met the rule.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo="Take care of each certificate in the list.">
    On the student's row, you can download the PDF, open the validation page, and resend the email. Under **More actions**, you choose **Generate again** or **Revoke**.
  </Passo>
</Passos>

## Good to know

:::tip You can change it for each mentorship
Your brand applies to all certificates, but you can change the logo, color, and signature on a specific mentorship's certificate.
:::

:::info Asked for the student's ID or signature?
If the certificate asks for the student's ID or signature, it only goes out after the student does it. In the list, the status shows as **Waiting for ID** or **Waiting for signature**.
:::

## Reference

| File | Accepted format |
| --- | --- |
| Logo | Transparent PNG or PDF. Image up to 2 MB, PDF up to 10 MB |
| Signature image | Transparent PNG or PDF. Image up to 2 MB, PDF up to 10 MB |

No signature image? Turn on **Use handwritten lettering instead of an image**: the platform writes the name in a signature font.

| Filter in **Issued** | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Not opened** | Certificates sent that the student hasn't downloaded yet |
| **Waiting for ID** | Students who haven't entered their ID yet |
| **Waiting for signature** | Students who haven't signed in their area yet |
| **Failed** | Certificates that weren't generated |
| **Revoked** | Certificates you canceled |

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>The student says they didn't get the email. How do I resend it?</summary>

On the student's row, use **Resend email**. You can also **Download PDF** and send it to them.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I issued one by mistake. Can I cancel it?</summary>

Yes. Under **More actions**, use **Revoke**. You can write a reason (optional, only you see it). The validation page then shows "invalid" and the student loses the download. If you change your mind, use **Reactivate** on the certificate's row.

</details>

<details>
<summary>A certificate failed or is waiting for the ID. What do I do?</summary>

If it failed, click **Try again** on the row. If it's **Waiting for ID**, the student needs to enter their ID; then click **Issue now** on the row. If it's **Waiting for signature**, only the student can sign, in their own area.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I get the list into a spreadsheet?</summary>

Click **Export list**, at the top of the issued screen. The platform downloads a file with the mentorship's certificates.

</details>
