Prepare the certificate once and turn it on. From then on, anyone who completes the mentorship gets the certificate by email, automatically.

<Aula slug="certificados" />

## Step by step

<Passos>
  <Passo titulo={<>In the menu, open <strong>Certificates</strong>, find the mentorship, and click <strong>Create certificate</strong>.</>}>
    ![Certificates screen with the list of mentorships and the Create certificate button](/img/guia/certificados/01-central.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>In <strong>Design</strong>, choose the look.</>}>
    The **Live preview**, on the side, shows the result right away.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>In <strong>Texts</strong>, enter the course <strong>Workload</strong> (hours).</>}>
    Without the hours, the certificate can't be turned on.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>In <strong>Signature</strong>, upload an image of your signature or use handwritten lettering.</>} />
  <Passo titulo={<>In <strong>When to issue</strong>, check the rule.</>}>
    The default is **When 100% of the steps are completed**. See the other options in Reference.

    ![When to issue step with the three rules and the certificate preview](/img/guia/certificados/09-quando.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Click <strong>Activate certificate</strong>.</>}>
    Done. Anyone who completes the mentorship gets the certificate by email.
  </Passo>
</Passos>

## Good to know

:::caution Turning it on also issues it to people who finished before
If a student had already completed the mentorship, they get the certificate as soon as you turn it on. The screen tells you how many students that is first.
:::

:::tip Until you turn it on, it's a draft
You can leave and come back later: the certificate stays as a **Draft** and isn't issued to anyone.
:::

## Reference

| In **When to issue** | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **When 100% of the steps are completed** | The certificate goes out on its own when the student finishes everything (recommended) |
| **When part of the steps is completed** | You set the percentage, for example 80% |
| **When specific modules are completed** | You choose how many modules, or which module, counts |
| **Issue automatically on completion** | When turned off, you issue it manually from the issued certificates screen |
| **Notify the student by email** | Sends the email with the certificate link |
| **Ask for the student ID before issuing** | The student enters it once and it's saved in their profile |

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>I turned off the certificate. Do people who already got it lose it?</summary>

No. Certificates already issued stay valid. Only new ones stop being issued.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I have several mentorships. Do I need to create a certificate for each one?</summary>

No. In **Default certificate**, turn on **Use for programs without their own certificate**. Mentorships without their own certificate start issuing this one. For this, the certificate must be active and use the 100% rule or the percentage-of-steps rule.

</details>

<details>
<summary>A student finished and didn't get it. What do I do?</summary>

Open **Issued** on the mentorship and use **Issue to a student**. See [Brand and issued certificates](/docs/cursos/certificados/marca-e-emitidos).

</details>
