Prepare the certificate once and turn it on. From then on, anyone who completes the mentorship gets the certificate by email, automatically.

<Aula slug="certificados" />

## Step by step

<Passos>
  <Passo titulo={<>In the menu, open <strong>Certificates</strong>, find the mentorship, and click <strong>Create certificate</strong>.</>}>
    Use **Search program** if you have many. The mentorships that need you show up first, in the **Need you** group.

    ![Certificates screen with the counters, the search, and the list of mentorships](/img/guia/certificados/01-central.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>In <strong>Design</strong>, choose the style and the color.</>}>
    Pick **Classic**, **Modern**, or **Minimal**, and an **Accent color**. The **Live preview**, on the side, shows the result right away. Want to use your own artwork? See [Use your own artwork and a default certificate](/docs/cursos/certificados/minha-arte-e-padrao).
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>In <strong>Texts</strong>, enter the <strong>Workload</strong>.</>}>
    The title and the main text are already filled in. In the texts, chips like **+ student name** and **+ program** are replaced with each student's details. Without the hours, the certificate can't be turned on, because the text uses the workload. In **Certificate language**, choose Portuguese (Brazil), English, or Spanish.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>In <strong>Signature</strong>, check who signs.</>}>
    If you already saved your brand, the signature comes filled in. If you want, upload another image or use handwritten lettering.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>In <strong>When to issue</strong>, check the rule.</>}>
    The default is **When 100% of the steps are completed**. See the other options in Reference.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Click <strong>Activate certificate</strong>.</>}>
    If the **Activate the new certificate?** window appears, click **Activate now**. Done: anyone who completes the mentorship gets the certificate by email.

    ![Editor with the four steps ready and the Activate certificate button](/img/guia/certificados/02-editor.jpg)
  </Passo>
</Passos>

## Good to know

:::tip With your brand saved, almost everything is already done
After you save your brand (see the next page), a new certificate starts with **3 of 4 steps ready**. Only the workload is missing. The **X of 4 steps ready** counter is at the top of the screen.
:::

:::caution Turning it on also issues it to people who finished before
If a student had already completed the mentorship, they get the certificate as soon as you turn it on. The confirmation window tells you how many students that is.
:::

:::info No need to save
Your changes are saved on their own. Next to the title, you see **Saved**. Until you turn it on, the certificate stays as a **Draft** and isn't issued to anyone. To see how it will look, click **Download sample PDF**.
:::

## Reference

| In **When to issue** | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **When 100% of the steps are completed** | The certificate goes out on its own when the student finishes everything (recommended) |
| **When part of the steps is completed** | You set the percentage, for example 80% |
| **When specific modules are completed** | You choose how many modules count, or which module |
| **Issue automatically on completion** | If turned off, you issue it by hand from the issued certificates screen |
| **Notify the student by email** | Sends the email with the certificate link |
| **Ask for the student ID before issuing** | The student enters it once and it stays saved on their profile |

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>I turned the certificate off. Do people who already got it lose it?</summary>

No. Certificates already issued stay valid. Only new ones stop. If it was the default certificate, it no longer is: mentorships that used the default are left without a certificate.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I have several mentorships. Do I need to create a certificate for each one?</summary>

No. You can choose one certificate as the default for mentorships that don't have their own. See [Use your own artwork and a default certificate](/docs/cursos/certificados/minha-arte-e-padrao).

</details>

<details>
<summary>A student finished and didn't get it. What do I do?</summary>

Open **Issued** on the mentorship and use **Issue to a student**. See [Brand and issued certificates](/docs/cursos/certificados/marca-e-emitidos).

</details>
