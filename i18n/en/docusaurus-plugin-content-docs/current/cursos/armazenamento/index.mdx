**Storage is the library for your videos and files. You upload once and use them in any lesson.**

- **Upload videos and files** from your computer or from Google Drive.
- **Organize them in folders**, one for each course or module.
- **See where each video is used** before deleting it, and recover it from the **Trash** if you change your mind.
