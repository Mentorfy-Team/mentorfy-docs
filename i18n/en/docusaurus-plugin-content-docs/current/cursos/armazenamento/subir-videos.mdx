Before building your lessons, upload your videos to **Storage**. The platform prepares each video to play well on any internet connection.

<Aula slug="armazenamento" />

## Step by step

<Passos>
  <Passo titulo={<>In the menu, open <strong>Storage</strong> and click <strong>Upload Files</strong>.</>} />
  <Passo titulo={<>Choose <strong>From Computer</strong> and select the video.</>}>
    If the video is on Google Drive, choose **From Google Drive**.

    ![Upload Files menu with the options From Computer and From Google Drive](/img/guia/armazenamento/02-menu-upload.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo="Follow the upload.">
    The progress shows up in **Files in progress**. You can keep using the platform in the meantime.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo="Done: the video shows up in the library, with its length.">
    After the upload, the platform still processes the video for a few moments before it's ready.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo="Click the video to see its details.">
    In **Video Details** you can watch it and, in **AI Generation**, create a transcript, subtitles, a quiz, and more.

    ![Video Details screen with the player and AI Generation](/img/guia/armazenamento/06-detalhes.jpg)
  </Passo>
</Passos>

## Good to know

:::info The space used shows at the top of the screen
The bar shows how much of your space is already used. It counts the original video and the versions in different qualities (1080p, 720p, 480p) that the platform creates on its own. Need more? Use **Increase storage**.
:::

:::tip AI Generation uses credits
Each **Generate** button shows how many credits it uses. Your balance shows in **Available credits**, on the same screen.
:::

## Reference

| What | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Supported videos | MP4, WebM, AVI, MOV, WMV, MKV, FLV, M4V, 3GP |
| Other files (PDF, images…) | up to 5 GB each |
| Where to upload from | **From Computer** or **From Google Drive** |
| Created with AI from the video | Transcript, Subtitles, Shorts, E-book, Mind Map, Quiz, Dubbing |

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>I got "Insufficient storage". What do I do?</summary>

You've run out of space. Delete old videos or use **Increase storage**. Heads up: what's in the **Trash** still takes up space. To free it up right away, open the **Trash** and empty it.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I put the video in a lesson?</summary>

In the mentorship editor, create a **Player de Áudio e Vídeo** (Audio and Video Player) step and choose the video in **From storage**. See [Build the modules and lessons](/docs/cursos/mentorias/modulos-e-aulas).

</details>
