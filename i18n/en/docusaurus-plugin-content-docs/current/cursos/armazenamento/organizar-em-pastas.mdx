With lots of videos, organize everything in folders: one for each course or module. Moving a video to another folder doesn't remove it from the lessons.

<Aula slug="armazenamento-p2-pastas" />

## Step by step

<Passos>
  <Passo titulo={<>In Storage, click <strong>New Folder</strong>.</>}>
    Give it a name, like "Leadership Lessons", and click **Create**.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>On the video, open the actions menu (the three dots) and choose <strong>Move</strong>.</>}>
    ![Video actions menu with View details, Watch, Download, Copy URL, Rename, Move, and Delete](/img/guia/armazenamento-p2-pastas/04-acoes.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Choose the destination folder and click <strong>Move to</strong> with its name.</>}>
    To take the video out of any folder, choose **Home**.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo="Done: the video is now in the folder.">
    It keeps working in every lesson where it already was.
  </Passo>
</Passos>

## Good to know

:::tip Before deleting, see where the video is used
When you click **Delete**, the platform shows which lessons use the video (for example, **Used in 1 lesson**).

![Move to trash? window with the notice Used in 1 lesson](/img/guia/armazenamento-p2-pastas/09-excluir-uso.jpg)
:::

:::info Delete sends it to the Trash
The item stays in the **Trash** for 30 days and can be restored. During that time, students keep watching it in the lessons. After that, it is deleted for good and disappears from the lessons.
:::

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>I deleted a video by mistake. How do I get it back?</summary>

Click **Trash**, at the top of Storage, and then **Restore** on the item. It goes back to the folder it came from. If that folder no longer exists, it goes back to the start of Storage.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What happens if I use "Delete forever" or "Empty trash"?</summary>

The item is deleted right away and it can't be undone. If the video was in a lesson, the lesson is left without that video. The screen tells you how many lessons will be affected before you confirm.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What if I delete a folder with videos inside?</summary>

The whole folder goes to the **Trash**, with everything inside it. The screen shows how many videos, files, and subfolders it has before you confirm. Restoring the folder brings everything back.

</details>
