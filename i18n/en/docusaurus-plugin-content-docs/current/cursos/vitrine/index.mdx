**The showcase is the first screen the student sees when entering your member area. It works like a streaming catalog: categories are the shelves, and mentorships appear inside them, each with its own cover.**

- **Organize in categories**: shelves like "Start here" or "Leadership", and even subcategories inside them.
- **Decide what shows up**: hide a shelf or a mentorship, close new enrollments, or let people know something is under construction.
- **Check how it looks**: in the menu, **View Showcase** opens the showcase (it asks for a student login).

:::info What the student sees
The student sees the mentorships they already have and also the ones they haven't bought yet (those appear locked), unless you hide them. See [Control what shows up in the showcase](/docs/cursos/vitrine/controlar-o-que-aparece).
:::
