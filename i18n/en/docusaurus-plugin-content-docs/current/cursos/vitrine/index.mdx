**The showcase is the first screen the student sees when entering your member area. It works like a streaming catalog: categories are the shelves, and mentorships appear inside them, each with its own cover.**

You do all of this in one place: **My Mentorships**. There, two icon buttons at the top switch the view: **Showcase** (the shelves, as the student sees them) and **Table** (a list with the status of each mentorship).

- **Organize in categories**: shelves like "Start here" or "Leadership", and even subcategories inside them.
- **Decide what shows up**: make a mentorship visible, under construction, or hidden, and hide a whole category.
- **Publish when you're ready**: changes stay as a draft until you click **Publish**.
- **Check how it looks**: the **Preview** button, at the top, shows the showcase as the student sees it. The **View Student Area** button, in the header, opens the student area in another tab, already signed in.

:::info What the student sees
The student sees the mentorships that are **Visible** and **Under construction**. The **Hidden** ones are seen only by you and your team. See [Control what shows up in the showcase](/docs/cursos/vitrine/controlar-o-que-aparece).
:::
