Categories are the shelves of your showcase: they make everything easy to find. Create the shelf, then put the mentorships inside it.

<Aula slug="categorias-vitrine" />

## Step by step

<Passos>
  <Passo titulo={<>In the menu, open <strong>Showcase Categories</strong> and click <strong>New Category</strong>.</>}>
    ![Showcase Categories screen with the list of categories and the No category area](/img/guia/categorias-vitrine/01-categorias.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Give it a name and click <strong>Create Category</strong>.</>}>
    Done, the new shelf shows up in the list.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo="On the mentorship that goes on this shelf, open the three-dot menu.">
    Mentorships that don't have a shelf yet are in **No category**, at the end of the list.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Choose <strong>Move to</strong> and then the category.</>}>
    ![Mentorship menu with Move to open and the list of categories](/img/guia/categorias-vitrine/07-mover.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo="Done: the mentorship is now inside the category.">
    The showcase organizes itself.
  </Passo>
</Passos>

## Good to know

:::tip One mentorship on two shelves
In the mentorship menu, **Add to another category** puts the mentorship on a new shelf without removing it from the ones it's already on. **Move to** takes it out of where it is and moves it to the new one.
:::

:::info Empty categories don't show up
The student only sees shelves that have at least one mentorship they can see. An empty category disappears from the showcase.
:::

:::tip No ideas for categories?
Click **Generate with AI**, tell it a bit about your mentorships, and the AI suggests categories. You check them before applying.
:::

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>How do I change the name or order of the categories?</summary>

To rename, double-click the name. To change the order, click and drag the category or the mentorship.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I create a subcategory?</summary>

Click the category's gear icon and, in **Parent Category (optional)**, choose the category above it. Then click **Save**. Leave it empty to keep it as a main category.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What happens if I delete a category?</summary>

The category and its subcategories are deleted. The mentorships are not deleted: they just leave that shelf. If they aren't on another one, they go back to **No category**.

</details>
