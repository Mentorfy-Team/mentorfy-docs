Categories are the shelves of your showcase: they make each mentorship easy to find. Create the shelf, put the mentorships inside it, and publish.

<Aula slug="categorias-vitrine" />

## Step by step

<Passos>
  <Passo titulo={<>In <strong>My Mentorships</strong>, open the <strong>Showcase</strong> view and click <strong>New category</strong>.</>}>
    The **Showcase** view is one of the two icon buttons at the top (the other is **Table**). **New category** is in the bottom bar.

    ![Showcase view of My Mentorships with the bottom bar and the New category button](/img/guia/categorias-vitrine/01-vitrine.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Give it a <strong>Name</strong> and click <strong>Create category</strong>.</>}>
    The new category shows up at the end of the showcase, empty. An empty category doesn't show up for the student.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>In the category title, click <strong>+ Program</strong>.</>}>
    The **Add programs** panel opens. Use the search, or the **All** / **No category** filter, to find the mentorships.

    ![Add programs panel with the search, the filter, and the list of mentorships to tick](/img/guia/categorias-vitrine/06-adicionar.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Tick the mentorships and click <strong>Add N programs</strong>.</>}>
    A mentorship can be in more than one category: the ones already in other categories stay there and also show up here. Don't have the mentorship yet? Click **Create new program**: it goes straight into this category.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo="Set the order.">
    The arrows move the category up and down the showcase. **Reorder** arranges the mentorships inside it.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Click <strong>Publish</strong> and confirm with <strong>Publish now</strong>.</>}>
    Until you publish, nothing changes for the student. The **Publish for your students?** window shows a summary of what will change. After you confirm, it can take a few minutes to show up in the student app.
  </Passo>
</Passos>

## Good to know

:::info Everything stays a draft until you publish
Each change goes into the draft. At the top you see how many changes are unpublished (for example, **1 unpublished change**) and the **Discard** button, which takes everything back to what the student sees today. The exception is a mentorship's cover: when you change it, the student already sees the new cover.
:::

:::info No category
Mentorships that aren't in any category show up for the student under **Other Products**. To take a mentorship out of the showcase, hide it (see [Control what shows up in the showcase](/docs/cursos/vitrine/controlar-o-que-aparece)).
:::

:::tip Want help getting started?
In the bottom bar, **Organize with AI** helps you build the categories. In the **Table** view, the left panel lists the categories, with the **AI** and **+ New** buttons.
:::

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>How do I rename a category or create a subcategory?</summary>

Click the gear icon next to the category title. **Rename** changes the name. **Move inside…** puts the category inside another one, and **Top level** brings it back to the start of the showcase.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What happens if I delete a category?</summary>

In the gear menu, click **Delete category**. The category and its subcategories leave the showcase, but the mentorships are not deleted. The ones that aren't in another category go to **Other Products**. The window also lets you choose another category to receive the mentorships. Like any change, the deletion only reaches the student when you publish.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I made a mistake. Can I undo it?</summary>

Yes, as long as you haven't published. After many changes a notice with **Undo** shows up, and the **Discard** button at the top takes everything back to what is published.

</details>
