In the showcase, you decide what the student sees: you can hide a whole shelf, hide a mentorship, close new enrollments, or let people know something is under construction.

<Aula slug="categorias-p2-visibilidade" />

## Step by step

<Passos>
  <Passo titulo={<>In <strong>Showcase Categories</strong>, click the category's gear icon.</>} />
  <Passo titulo={<>Turn on <strong>Hide from showcase</strong> and click <strong>Save</strong>.</>}>
    The category gets the **Hidden from showcase** label. People who already bought one of its mentorships keep seeing that mentorship.

    ![Category Settings window with the Hide from showcase option](/img/guia/categorias-p2-visibilidade/02-engrenagem.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo="For a single mentorship, use the icons on its row.">
    The eye shows or hides it, the padlock blocks new enrollments, and the last icon turns on construction mode. See what each one does in Reference.

    ![Mentorship row with the eye, padlock, and construction icons](/img/guia/categorias-p2-visibilidade/05-produto.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo="Click the padlock to block new enrollments.">
    People who already have access keep it, and no one new gets in. Click again to open it up.
  </Passo>
</Passos>

## Good to know

:::tip Hover over the icon
Each icon shows what is turned on, for example **Visible** or **Hidden**, and what happens when you click it.
:::

:::caution Under construction applies to everyone
Construction mode blocks access for everyone, including people who already bought it. Use it only while you're building.
:::

## Reference

| Control | What the student sees |
| --- | --- |
| **Hide from showcase** (category gear icon) | The shelf disappears. People who already have access to one of its mentorships keep seeing only that mentorship |
| Eye: **Visible** / **Hidden** | When hidden, the mentorship only shows up for people who already bought it |
| Padlock: **Block new enrollments** | People who already have access keep it; no one new gets in |
| **Enable construction mode** | The cover shows up, but no one can open it for now |

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>I hid a mentorship. Do students who already bought it lose access?</summary>

No. Hiding only takes the mentorship out of view for people who didn't buy it. People who already have access keep seeing and watching it.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I want to take a mentorship down for good. Should I hide it or delete it?</summary>

Hiding is better: you can undo it with one click. Deleting the mentorship removes access for all students and it can't be recovered.

</details>
