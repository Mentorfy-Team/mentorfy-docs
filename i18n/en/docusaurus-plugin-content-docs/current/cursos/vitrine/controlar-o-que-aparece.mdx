In the showcase, you decide what the student sees: each mentorship can be visible, under construction, or hidden, and a whole category can be hidden.

<Aula slug="categorias-p2-visibilidade" />

## Step by step

<Passos>
  <Passo titulo={<>In <strong>My Mentorships</strong>, in the <strong>Table</strong> view, click the mentorship's <strong>Status</strong>.</>}>
    The **Status** column shows how the student sees each mentorship.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>In the <strong>How students see it</strong> menu, choose an option.</>}>
    The change goes into the draft and a notice with **Undo** shows up. The options are in Reference.

    ![How students see it menu open in the Status column, with Visible, Under construction, and Hidden](/img/guia/categorias-p2-visibilidade/02-status.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>To hide a whole category, open the <strong>Showcase</strong> view and click the category's gear icon.</>}>
    Choose **Hide from students**. The category gets the **Hidden from students** label. To go back, choose **Show to students**.

    ![Category gear menu with Rename, Move inside, Hide from students, and Delete category](/img/guia/categorias-p2-visibilidade/05-menu.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Click <strong>Preview</strong> to check.</>}>
    The button is next to **Edit**, at the top. Preview shows the showcase as the student sees it: the hidden category disappears, and mentorships under construction show the **Available soon** notice. To keep editing, click **Edit**.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Click <strong>Publish</strong> and confirm with <strong>Publish now</strong>.</>}>
    Only then does the change reach the student. It can take a few minutes to show up in their app.
  </Passo>
</Passos>

## Good to know

:::tip Find what's hidden
At the top of the **Table**, the **Visible**, **Hidden**, **No cover**, and **No students** filters help you find what you're looking for. **More filters** opens other options.
:::

:::caution Hiding does not remove access from students who already have it
A **Hidden** mentorship and a hidden category leave the showcase for people who don't have access yet. A student who already has access to the mentorship still sees it and can open it.
:::

:::info Under construction: the student sees it but can't open it
Use it to show the cover of a mentorship that's still being built. The student sees the card, but can only open it once you change it to **Visible**.
:::

## Reference

| Status | What the student sees |
| --- | --- |
| **Visible** | The student sees it and can open it |
| **Under construction** | The student sees the card but can't open it yet |
| **Hidden** | It leaves the showcase. Students who already have access still see it |

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>I hid a mentorship and it still shows up for the student. Why?</summary>

The change only reaches the student after **Publish**. If it's in more than one category, check in **Preview**: a hidden mentorship doesn't show up in any of them.

</details>

<details>
<summary>The mentorship is Visible, but the student doesn't see it. Why?</summary>

In the **Table**, a yellow icon next to the mentorship explains the reason when you hover over it. For example, its category is hidden or it's only in hidden categories.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I want to take a mentorship down for good. Should I hide it or delete it?</summary>

Hiding is better: you can undo it with one click. Deleting a mentorship is forever and the screen warns you it can't be recovered (see [Create your first mentorship](/docs/cursos/mentorias/criar-mentoria)).

</details>
