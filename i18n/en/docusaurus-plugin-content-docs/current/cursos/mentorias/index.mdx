**Everything starts in My Mentorships: the mentorship is the structure that holds your lessons, and the rest of the platform connects to it.**

- **Create the mentorship** in less than a minute.
- **Build the modules**: each module groups steps (video lessons, text, files, tasks, quizzes…).
- **Ask the AI for help**: you describe the topic and it builds a module with the steps ready for you to review.
