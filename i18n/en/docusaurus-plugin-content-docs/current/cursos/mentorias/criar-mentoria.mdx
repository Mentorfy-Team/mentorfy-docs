The mentorship is the product your student buys. Create the mentorship first: then you add the modules and lessons inside it.

<Aula slug="minhas-mentorias" />

## Step by step

<Passos>
  <Passo titulo={<>In the menu, open <strong>My Mentorships</strong> and click <strong>New Mentoring</strong>.</>}>
    The button is in the top right corner.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Choose <strong>Manual</strong>.</>}>
    **Manual** creates it from scratch, with full freedom. **MentorAI** is the AI-guided build.

    ![New Mentoring window with the options MentorAI and Manual](/img/guia/minhas-mentorias/02-modo.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Choose <strong>Single Product</strong>.</>}>
    It creates one complete mentorship at a time. **Multiple Products** is for creating several at once.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>In <strong>Mentoring Name</strong>, give it a name.</>}>
    This is the name the student sees. You can change it later.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>In <strong>Mentoring Type</strong>, choose the format.</>}>
    **Online Course** is the format with video lessons. See the other types in Reference, just below.

    ![New Mentoring - Manual form with the four types](/img/guia/minhas-mentorias/06-tipo.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Click <strong>Create Mentoring</strong>.</>}>
    Done: the mentorship opens right away in the editor, so you can build the modules.
  </Passo>
</Passos>

## Good to know

:::tip Mentorship and product are the same thing
On some screens you'll see "product" instead of "mentorship". It's the same item.
:::

:::info Short on time?
After you create it, the AI can draft a whole module for you. See [Create a module with AI](/docs/cursos/mentorias/modulo-com-ia).
:::

## Reference

| Type | What it's for (screen text) |
| --- | --- |
| **Kanban** | Card-based management methodology to optimize workflow |
| **A. B. / Reels** | Kanban variation that makes it easier to visualize overall progress |
| **Online Course** | Videos to transfer knowledge from subject-matter experts |
| **Continued Mentoring** | Long-term relationship to develop specific skills |

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>Where do I find the mentorship after creating it?</summary>

In **My Mentorships**, in the list of cards. Click **Edit** to go back to the editor. The card menu also has **Mentoring Settings**, **View certificates**, **Duplicate**, and **Export content**.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What happens if I delete a mentorship?</summary>

From the card menu, **Delete mentoring** deletes the mentorship and removes access for all its students. The screen itself warns you: it can't be recovered. If you only want to take the mentorship out of view, hide it in the showcase (see [Control what shows up](/docs/cursos/vitrine/controlar-o-que-aparece)).

</details>
