The mentorship is the product your student buys. Create the mentorship first: then you add the modules and lessons inside it.

<Aula slug="minhas-mentorias" />

## Step by step

<Passos>
  <Passo titulo={<>In the menu, open <strong>My Mentorships</strong> and click <strong>New program</strong>.</>}>
    It's the orange button. In the **Table** view, it's in the top right corner. In the **Showcase** view, it's in the bottom bar, next to **New category**.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Choose <strong>Manual</strong>.</>}>
    **Manual** (Full control, about 1 minute) creates it from scratch, with full freedom. It's the path on this page. The other two options use AI: **Create with AI, step by step** (about 6 minutes) asks one question at a time, and **MentorAI** (Recommended, about 8 minutes) is the AI-guided build.

    ![New Mentoring window with the three ways to create: AI step by step, MentorAI and Manual](/img/guia/minhas-mentorias/02-modo.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Choose <strong>Single Product</strong>.</>}>
    It creates one mentorship at a time. **Multiple Products** is for creating several at once.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>In <strong>Mentoring Name</strong>, give it a name.</>}>
    This is the name the student sees. You can change it later.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>In <strong>Mentoring Type</strong>, choose the format.</>}>
    **Online Course** is the format with video lessons. See the other types in Reference, just below.

    ![New Mentoring - Manual form with the four types and the Create Mentoring button](/img/guia/minhas-mentorias/06-tipo.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Click <strong>Create Mentoring</strong>.</>}>
    Done: the mentorship opens right away in the editor (**Mentoring Steps**), and the **Create first module** button is waiting for you. If you want, before creating, use **Link to a group**: the mentorship joins the groups you tick.
  </Passo>
</Passos>

## Good to know

:::tip Mentorship and product are the same thing
On some screens you'll see "product" instead of "mentorship". It's the same item.
:::

:::info Under construction: the student sees it but can't open it
In the list, the new mentorship shows the status **Under construction**. The student sees the card but can't open it yet. When it's ready, change the status to **Visible** in the **Status** column.
:::

:::info Short on time?
After you create it, the AI can draft a whole module for you. See [Create a module with AI](/docs/cursos/mentorias/modulo-com-ia).
:::

## Reference

| Type | What it's for (screen text) |
| --- | --- |
| **Kanban** | Card-based management methodology to optimize workflow |
| **A. B. / Reels** | Kanban variation that makes it easier to visualize overall progress |
| **Online Course** | Videos to transfer knowledge from subject-matter experts |
| **Continued Mentoring** | Long-term relationship to develop specific skills |

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>Where do I find the mentorship after creating it?</summary>

In **My Mentorships**, in the **Table** view, each mentorship is a row. Click the pencil (**Edit content**) to go back to the editor. The row's **...** menu also has **Configure**, **Edit content**, **Journey**, **Appointments**, **Certificates**, **Access, price and delivery**, **Duplicate**, and **Export**.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What happens if I delete a mentorship?</summary>

In the row's **...** menu, click **Delete**, then **Proceed**, then **Confirm Deletion**. The mentorship is deleted for good: the screen itself warns you that it can't be recovered and that mentees lose access. If there are still clients linked, the screen tells you it couldn't be removed. If you only want to take the mentorship out of view, hide it in the showcase (see [Control what shows up](/docs/cursos/vitrine/controlar-o-que-aparece)).

</details>
