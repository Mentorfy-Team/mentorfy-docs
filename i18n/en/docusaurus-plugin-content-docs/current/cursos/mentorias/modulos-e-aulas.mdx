With the mentorship created, it's time for the content. **Modules** group the lessons, and each lesson is a **step**: video, text, files, tasks, and much more.

<Aula slug="mentorias-p2-modulos" />

## Step by step

<Passos>
  <Passo titulo={<>In the mentorship editor, click <strong>New Module</strong>.</>}>
    Then click **Edit** on the module and give it a name.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>In the module, click <strong>Add step</strong>.</>}>
    The list of tools opens, grouped by type. See them all in Reference.

    ![Course Building Tools list](/img/guia/mentorias-p2-modulos/06-tipos-etapa.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>For a video lesson, choose <strong>Player de Áudio e Vídeo</strong> (Audio and Video Player) and click <strong>Add to Course</strong>.</>} />
  <Passo titulo={<>Click <strong>Edit</strong> on the step, give it a title, and choose the video in <strong>From storage</strong>.</>}>
    You can also **Upload from computer** or use an **External link**.

    ![Video window with the From storage tab open](/img/guia/mentorias-p2-modulos/10-armazenamento.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Click <strong>Save</strong> on the step.</>} />
  <Passo titulo={<>Finally, click <strong>Save changes</strong>, at the top of the editor.</>}>
    Done: the lesson is now in the module.
  </Passo>
</Passos>

## Good to know

:::caution Don't forget the last save
Saving the step is not enough. Click **Save changes**, at the top of the editor, before you leave.
:::

:::tip Upload the videos first
Building lessons is faster when the videos are already in **Storage**. See [Upload your lesson videos](/docs/cursos/armazenamento/subir-videos).
:::

## Reference

The **Add step** tools are split into groups:

| Group | What it's for (screen text) |
| --- | --- |
| **Course Content** | Tools used to present content to students (text, video, downloadable files, embedded content…) |
| **Student Interaction** | Tools that allow students to actively participate in the course (task list, file submission…) |
| **Activities and Assessments** | Tools to assess and exercise student knowledge (like the multiple-choice quiz) |
| **Feedback and Engagement** | Tools to understand student experience and satisfaction |
| **Organization** | Tools to structure and organize content |

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>I deleted a module or a step by mistake. Can I get it back?</summary>

Yes. In the mentorship editor, click **Restore Changes**. Deleted modules and steps show up there, and when you restore them, the students' answers come back too.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I add a quiz to the lesson?</summary>

Use the **Perguntas de Múltipla Escolha** (Multiple Choice Questions) step, in **Activities and Assessments**. It pulls the questions from the Question Bank. See [Use the questions in a lesson](/docs/cursos/banco-de-questoes/usar-numa-aula).

</details>
