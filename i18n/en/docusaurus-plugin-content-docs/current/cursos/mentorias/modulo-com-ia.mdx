Don't know where to start a module? Describe the topic: the AI suggests the title and the steps, you adjust them, and it even writes the content of each step.

<Aula slug="mentorias-p3-ia" />

## Step by step

<Passos>
  <Passo titulo={<>In the mentorship editor, click <strong>Create with AI</strong>.</>} />
  <Passo titulo={<>In <strong>Where to create</strong>, choose <strong>New module</strong> and write the topic in <strong>Instructions for the AI</strong>.</>}>
    The clearer, the better. Example: "A module on how to give feedback to your team: why it matters, a step by step, and a hands-on exercise at the end."

    ![Create with AI window with the instructions filled in](/img/guia/mentorias-p3-ia/03-instrucoes.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>If you want, attach material in <strong>Context documents</strong>.</>}>
    This is optional. The AI uses the material to get closer to your way of teaching.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Click <strong>Generate suggestion</strong> and review it.</>}>
    The AI suggests the title and the steps. You can change the title, type, and order, delete steps, or click **Add step**.

    ![AI suggestion with the title and four steps](/img/guia/mentorias-p3-ia/05-sugestao.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Keep <strong>Fill content with AI</strong> turned on and click <strong>Create module</strong>.</>}>
    With this option on, the AI writes the content of each step for you.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo="Review the finished module.">
    It shows up in the editor with its steps. Read it, adjust what you want, and click **Save changes**.
  </Passo>
</Passos>

## Good to know

:::tip Already started a module?
In **Where to create**, choose **Add to a module** so the AI creates steps inside a module that already exists.
:::

:::caution Always review before releasing
The AI makes a draft. Check the text of each step before your students see it.
:::

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>I didn't like the suggestion. What now?</summary>

Click **Back**, improve the instructions, and click **Generate suggestion** again. Nothing is created until you click **Create module**.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I attach more than one file?</summary>

Yes. Drag or click the **Context documents** area and choose up to 5 PDF, DOCX, or TXT files, up to 10 MB each. When you reach the limit, the screen lets you know.

</details>
