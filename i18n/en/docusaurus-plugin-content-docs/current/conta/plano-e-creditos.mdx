In **My Account**, on the **Subscription** tab, you see everything about your account: the plan, how much you've used, your AI credits, and your add-ons.

<Aula slug="minha-conta" />

## Step by step

<Passos>
  <Passo titulo={<>Click your name, at the bottom of the side menu, and then <strong>My Subscription</strong>.</>}>
    The **My Account** screen opens on the **Subscription** tab.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>In <strong>My plan</strong>, see your plan and how long it's valid.</>}>
    ![Subscription tab with the plan, usage, and included features](/img/guia/minha-conta/assinatura.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>In <strong>Usage</strong>, check how many clients, mentorships, and teams you already use.</>}>
    Each row shows what you use and your plan's limit.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>In the <strong>AI Credits</strong> row, see how many credits you have left.</>}>
    To get more, click **Subscribe more**.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Scroll down to <strong>Add-ons</strong> to add features to your plan.</>}>
    ![Add-ons: AI Credits, Meeting, Extra Storage, and others](/img/guia/minha-conta/complementos.jpg)
  </Passo>
</Passos>

## Good to know

:::tip Colored bar
The bar in each row turns **yellow** near the limit and **red** when it's almost used up. For credits, the color warns you when your balance is low.
:::

:::info Free trial credits
During the trial, the 50 welcome credits show up as **One-off**, with a notice of how many days are left before they expire along with the trial.
:::

## Reference

**What the Subscription tab shows**

| Part of the screen | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **My plan** | The plan name, whether it is **Active**, **Free trial**, or **Expired**, and **Valid until**. |
| **Usage** | **Clients**, **Mentorings**, and **Teams**: how much you use and your plan's limit. |
| **AI Credits** | How many credits are still available. Below: **Monthly** (renews every month) and **One-off** (extras). |
| **Storage** | How much space your videos and files take up. |
| **Included Features** | What the plan includes, like **Custom Storefront** and **Community**. |
| **Add-ons** | Extras you add to your plan and cancel whenever you want. |

**Add-ons**

| Add-on | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **AI Credits Subscription** | AI credits renewed every month. |
| **Meeting** | Live meeting rooms, with recording. Some plans already include it. |
| **Extra Storage** | More space for videos and materials. |
| **Extra Mentoring** | Create more mentorships beyond your plan's limit. |
| **Community** | A space to bring your students together, with a feed, groups, and conversations. |

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>What uses AI credits?</summary>

AI Studio, video dubbing and, during the free trial, the Copilots. See the full table in [How much it costs](/docs/comece/quanto-custa).

</details>

<details>
<summary>If I cancel the AI Credits Subscription, do I lose my credits right away?</summary>

No. The subscription stays active until the end of the current period. After that date, the monthly credits are not renewed.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I cancel Extra Storage?</summary>

This add-on is changed or canceled by the sales team. Click **Talk to Sales**, on the **Extra Storage** card.

</details>

<details>
<summary>My plan shows as Expired. What now?</summary>

Click **Renew**, on the plan card, to keep using all the features.

</details>
