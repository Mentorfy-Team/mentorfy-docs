In AI Studio, you create your course cover in seconds, without needing a designer. The finished image becomes the product's cover or banner in one click.

<Aula slug="ai-studio" />

## Step by step

<Passos>
  <Passo titulo={<>In the menu, open <strong>AI Studio</strong>, on the <strong>Generate Image</strong> tab.</>} />
  <Passo titulo={<>In <strong>Describe the image</strong>, write the cover you want, in your own words.</>}>
    ![Describe the image field](/img/guia/ai-studio/02-descricao.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Pick the <strong>Style</strong> and the <strong>Image format</strong>.</>}>
    **Vertical product cover** is already selected.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Click <strong>Generate Image</strong>.</>}>
    In a few seconds, the cover is ready. It costs 5 credits.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>In <strong>Use image in product</strong>, pick the product.</>}>
    ![Use image in product](/img/guia/ai-studio/06-produto-escolhido.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Click <strong>Use as vertical cover</strong>.</>}>
    Done: the product already has its new cover.
  </Passo>
</Passos>

## Good to know

:::tip Tips for a good description
Be specific: setting, colors, light, and style. For example: "smiling mentor in a modern office, golden sunset light, orange and brown tones". Avoid generic descriptions, like "nice cover".
:::

:::info
Want a look similar to another image? Upload a **reference image** (optional). The AI takes inspiration from it.
:::

## Reference

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Style** | **Realistic**, **Artistic**, **Cartoon**, **3D Render**, **Watercolor**, **Pixel Art** |
| **Vertical product cover** | 10:16 ratio, matching the 900×1440 cover in the student area |
| **Horizontal product banner** | 12:5 ratio, matching the 2160×900 banner in the student area |
| **Reference image** | Up to 5 images, PNG, JPG, or WebP, up to 10 MB each |
| **Cost** | 5 credits per image |

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>It said "You are out of AI credits".</summary>

Each image costs 5 credits. Buy more in **Add credits**, at the top of AI Studio, or in **My Account**.

</details>

<details>
<summary>The generation failed. Did I lose my credits?</summary>

No. When the generation fails, the credits are refunded.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Where are the images I've already generated?</summary>

In the **Gallery** tab, along with your audio and videos. The most recent ones also show up below the result, in **Previously generated**.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I get back something I removed from the Gallery?</summary>

No. Removing from the Gallery deletes the material for good.

</details>
