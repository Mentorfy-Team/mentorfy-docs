Turn your lesson material into a podcast, with narrators talking to each other. AI writes the script for you, and you can adjust each line before generating the audio.

<Aula slug="ai-studio-p3-podcast" />

## Step by step

<Passos>
  <Passo titulo={<>In <strong>AI Studio</strong>, open the <strong>Podcast</strong> tab and leave <strong>AI Generates the Script</strong> selected.</>} />
  <Passo titulo={<>In <strong>Reference material</strong>, upload the lesson file and write the topic.</>}>
    PDF, Word (DOCX), TXT, or MD all work, and more than one file too. The topic goes in **Topic or additional context**.

    ![Uploading the material and the topic](/img/guia/ai-studio-p3-podcast/02-material.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Pick the length, the <strong>Podcast sentiment</strong>, and the <strong>Narrators</strong>.</>}>
    The length goes from 1 to 30 minutes. With **Add Narrator**, you add more voices.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Click <strong>Generate Script with AI</strong>.</>}>
    Before generating the audio, you can read and adjust each line.

    ![Generated script, line by line](/img/guia/ai-studio-p3-podcast/05-roteiro.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Click <strong>Generate Podcast</strong>.</>}>
    Done: the audio is ready to listen to and to save with **Download**.
  </Passo>
</Passos>

## Good to know

:::info
The podcast audio costs 2 credits for every 1,000 characters of the script (minimum of 2 credits). The longer the script, the higher the cost.
:::

:::tip
Already have the text ready? Choose **Manual Script** and write the lines yourself.
:::

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>It said "Add files or describe the topic."</summary>

AI needs a starting point. Upload at least one file or write the topic.

</details>

<details>
<summary>One line sounds odd. Do I need to generate everything again?</summary>

No. Edit the line right in the script before clicking **Generate Podcast**. The **AI** button next to the line makes the text sound more natural.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Where do I find my old podcasts?</summary>

In **Previous podcasts**, on the **Podcast** tab itself.

</details>
