In AI Studio, you turn text into narration. It's great for a lesson intro or a notice to your students.

<Aula slug="ai-studio-p2-audio" />

## Step by step

<Passos>
  <Passo titulo={<>In <strong>AI Studio</strong>, open the <strong>Generate Audio</strong> tab, in <strong>Text to Voice</strong>.</>} />
  <Passo titulo={<>In the <strong>Text</strong> field, write what you want narrated.</>}>
    Use punctuation: commas and periods become pauses and make the speech sound more natural.

    ![Text field in Text to Voice](/img/guia/ai-studio-p2-audio/02-texto.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Pick the voice's <strong>Gender</strong> and <strong>Area</strong>.</>}>
    With **Listen to this voice**, you hear it before generating.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Click <strong>Generate Audio</strong>.</>}>
    In a few seconds, the narration is ready to listen to.

    ![Finished narration in the player](/img/guia/ai-studio-p2-audio/04-pronto.jpg)
  </Passo>
</Passos>

## Good to know

:::info
It costs 1 credit for every 1,000 characters, rounded up (minimum of 1 credit). The estimate shows below the button.
:::

:::tip
Click **Naturalness** before generating: the AI adds pauses, emotion, and emphasis to your text, and the narration sounds more natural.
:::

## Reference

| Item | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Text** | Up to 5,000 characters per audio |
| **Gender** | **Female** or **Male** |
| **Area** | **Education**, **Corporate**, **Marketing**, **Podcast**, **Narrative**, **News** |
| **Cost** | 1 credit for every 1,000 characters (minimum 1) |

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>My text is longer than 5,000 characters.</summary>

Split it into parts and generate one audio for each part.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Where do I find the audio I've already generated?</summary>

Right below the result, in **Previously generated**, and in the **Gallery** tab.

</details>
