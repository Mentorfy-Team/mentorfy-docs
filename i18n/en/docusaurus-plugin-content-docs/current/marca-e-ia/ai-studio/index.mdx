**In AI Studio, AI creates your course material in seconds, with no designer or studio.**

- **Generate Image:** covers and banners for your products.
- **Generate Audio:** turn text into narration.
- **Podcast:** turn your lesson material into a conversation between two narrators.

Each creation uses AI credits. Your balance shows at the top of the page.
