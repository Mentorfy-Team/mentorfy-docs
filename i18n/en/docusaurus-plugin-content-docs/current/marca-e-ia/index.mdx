**Your brand in front of the student, and AI working behind the scenes.**

- **Brand Studio:** colors, logo, and layout for the student area, no code needed.
- **AI Studio:** covers, narrations, and podcasts created with AI.
- **My Copilot:** ask about your students and hand off day-to-day tasks.
- **Student Copilot:** an assistant that answers your students' questions at any time.

:::info
AI Studio uses the **AI credits** from your plan. On a paid plan, My Copilot and Student Copilot don't use credits; during the trial period, they do. You track and buy credits in **My Account**.
:::
