The Copilot doesn't just answer: it also does tasks for you, like giving or renewing a student's access. You ask in your own words, and it lets you know when it's done.

<Aula slug="copiloto-p2-executar" />

## Step by step

<Passos>
  <Passo titulo={<>In <strong>My Copilot</strong>, write your request the way you'd say it to someone on your team, and send it.</>}>
    Say who, what, and until when. For example: "Renew maria@exemplo.com's access to the Leadership in Practice product for 30 more days."

    ![Request written in the message box](/img/guia/copiloto-p2-executar/01-pedido.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>The Copilot finds the student and the product, does the task, and lets you know when it's done.</>}>
    ![Copilot confirms the renewal is done](/img/guia/copiloto-p2-executar/02-proposta.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Check the result in <strong>My Clients</strong>.</>}>
    The access is already valid until the new date.
  </Passo>
</Passos>

## Good to know

:::caution Simple tasks are done right away
Giving or renewing access, sending a password reset, and logging a student out of their devices are done directly, without asking for confirmation. Check your request (name, email, product, and deadline) before sending it.
:::

:::info Sensitive actions need your approval
Before sending a WhatsApp message, merging duplicate accounts, or deleting something (like a module, a group, or a scheduled task), the Copilot shows an **Approval required** card. Nothing happens until you click **Approve and run**. To cancel, click **Don't do it**.
:::

## Reference

| What it can do | Asks for approval? |
| --- | --- |
| Look up students, products, access, and numbers | No (read only) |
| Give or renew access, send a password reset, log out devices | No, does it directly |
| Create or change products and modules | No, does it directly |
| Send a WhatsApp message | Yes |
| Merge duplicate accounts | Yes |
| Delete a module, step, group, or scheduled task, or remove students from a group | Yes |

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>The approval card disappeared or says it expired.</summary>

An approval works only once and only for that data. If it expired, ask the Copilot to prepare the action again.

</details>

<details>
<summary>The Copilot did something wrong. How do I fix it?</summary>

Check the result on the right screen (for example, in **My Clients**) and fix it there, or ask the Copilot for the fix in a new message, like "set the student's access back to the previous date".

</details>

<details>
<summary>Does asking for tasks also use credits?</summary>

Not on a paid plan. During the trial period, each message uses 0.5 credit, whether it's a question or a request. The exception is sending a WhatsApp message through the Copilot: each send uses 0.5 credit on any plan.

</details>
