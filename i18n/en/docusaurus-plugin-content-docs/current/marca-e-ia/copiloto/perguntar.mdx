The Copilot knows your platform. Ask how your students, products, and access are doing, and it answers with data, in a chart.

<Aula slug="copiloto" />

## Step by step

<Passos>
  <Passo titulo={<>In the menu, open <strong>Copilot</strong> and then <strong>My Copilot</strong>.</>}>
    To start fresh, click **New Conversation**.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Write your question, the way you'd say it to someone on your team.</>}>
    For example: "Which products have the most active students? Show it in a chart."

    ![Question written in the message box](/img/guia/copiloto/02-pergunta.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Click send.</>}>
    The Copilot looks up your data and analyzes it.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>See the chart and read the summary at the end.</>}>
    ![Answer with a chart of the products](/img/guia/copiloto/04-resposta.jpg)
  </Passo>
</Passos>

## Good to know

:::tip Short questions work best
Ask for one thing at a time. If the question is too long, the Copilot may stop with the message "Iteration limit reached". Split it into two questions.
:::

:::info
On a paid plan, chatting with the Copilot doesn't use AI credits. During the trial period, each message uses 0.5 credit; if your balance runs out, the message box shows "You have no credits to send messages".
:::

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>How do I find an old conversation?</summary>

Click **History**. There you can search conversations, mark the ones you use most as **Favorites**, and organize them by **Categories**.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Does the Copilot only answer, or does it also do things?</summary>

It also does tasks for you, like renewing a student's access. See the next page.
