Always ask the Copilot the same questions? Save each one as a template and use it with one click, without typing everything again.

<Aula slug="copiloto-p3-templates" />

## Step by step

<Passos>
  <Passo titulo={<>In the corner of the message box, open the <strong>Template Library</strong>.</>}>
    ![Template Library open](/img/guia/copiloto-p3-templates/02-biblioteca.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Click <strong>New</strong>.</>} />
  <Passo titulo={<>Fill in the <strong>Title</strong>, write the question in <strong>Prompt</strong>, and pick a <strong>Category</strong>.</>}>
    The category can be anything: for example, Clients, Products, or Reports.

    ![Filled-in New Template form](/img/guia/copiloto-p3-templates/04-preenchido.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>If you use it a lot, check <strong>Favorite</strong>. Then click <strong>Create Template</strong>.</>} />
  <Passo titulo={<>When you need it, click <strong>Use</strong>.</>}>
    The question already shows up written in the message box. Just send it.
  </Passo>
</Passos>

## Good to know

:::tip
Leave the end of the question open so you can finish it on the spot. For example: "Look up information about the student: " and then paste the email.
:::

:::info
Templates are yours alone: each mentor sees only the ones they created.
:::

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>How do I find a template among many?</summary>

In the **Template Library**, use **Search templates...**, filter by category in **All categories**, or by the star, to see only your favorites.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I change or delete a template?</summary>

Yes. On the template's card, use **Edit** or **Delete**.

</details>
