Student Copilot is an assistant that answers your students' questions at any time, the way you teach it. It shows up as a floating button in the student area.

<Aula slug="copiloto-aluno" />

## Step by step

<Passos>
  <Passo titulo={<>In the menu, open <strong>Copilot</strong> and then <strong>Student Copilot</strong>.</>} />
  <Passo titulo={<>In <strong>Visual Customization</strong>, fill in the <strong>Display Name</strong> and pick the <strong>Primary Color</strong>.</>}>
    The **Button Preview** shows the button the student will see. If you leave the name empty, it shows "Copilot".

    ![The assistant's name and color, with the button preview](/img/guia/copiloto-aluno/03-nome-cor.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>In <strong>Copilot Instructions</strong>, tell it how to answer.</>}>
    To start fast, click one of the **Prompt Examples**: **General Support**, **Business Mentor**, or **Personal Development Coach**. Then adjust it in your own words.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>In <strong>Limits per Student</strong>, set the <strong>Daily Limit</strong> of messages.</>}>
    Use 0 for no limit.

    ![Message limits per student](/img/guia/copiloto-aluno/06-limites.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Click <strong>Save</strong>, in the top corner.</>} />
  <Passo titulo={<>When you want to make it available to students, turn on <strong>Copilot Status</strong>.</>}>
    When it's off, students don't see it. You can set everything up calmly before turning it on.
  </Passo>
</Passos>

## Good to know

:::tip
Be specific in the instructions. The more detailed, the better the answers.
:::

:::caution
To turn on the assistant, you need to have AI credits available. During the trial period, each student message uses credits from **your** account; on a paid plan, it doesn't. See usage in [Limits and usage](/docs/marca-e-ia/copiloto-do-aluno/limites-e-consumo).
:::

## Reference

| Field | What it accepts |
| --- | --- |
| **Display Name** | Any text. Empty = "Copilot" |
| **Primary Color** | One of the ready-made colors or a **Custom color** |
| **Copilot Avatar** | PNG, JPG, or GIF, up to 2 MB (128x128 px recommended) |
| **Daily / Weekly / Monthly Limit** | Number of messages per student. 0 = no limit |

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>I saved, but my students don't see the button. Why?</summary>

Check that **Copilot Status** is on: when it's off, students don't see the assistant. Also check the **Release Rules**: if you required days since registration or a specific product, the student can only use the assistant once they meet the rules.

</details>

<details>
<summary>A notice said I don't have credits.</summary>

With no credits available, you can't turn on the assistant, even on a paid plan. Buy more credits in **My Account** and try again.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Does the assistant answer with the content of my lessons?</summary>

It uses your instructions and the documents you upload to the **Knowledge Base**. See how to upload them on the next page.

</details>
