In the **Knowledge Base**, you give your material to Student Copilot: workbook, FAQ, student guide. That way, it answers with your content, and not only with what the AI already knows.

<Aula slug="copiloto-aluno-p2-base" />

## Step by step

<Passos>
  <Passo titulo={<>In <strong>Student Copilot</strong>, open the <strong>Knowledge Base</strong>.</>}>
    ![Knowledge Base open](/img/guia/copiloto-aluno-p2-base/02-aberta.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>If you want, fill in the <strong>Document Title (optional)</strong>.</>}>
    For example: "Student guide". It helps you recognize the file in the list.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Click <strong>Select File</strong> and choose the file.</>}>
    PDF, Word (DOCX), TXT, or MD all work.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Wait for the document to show as <strong>Ready</strong>.</>}>
    It takes a few seconds. From then on, the assistant uses this material in its answers.

    ![Document with the Ready badge](/img/guia/copiloto-aluno-p2-base/05-pronto.jpg)
  </Passo>
</Passos>

## Good to know

:::tip
Choose documents with well-organized text, like questions and answers or topics with headings. The assistant finds the answer more easily.
:::

:::info Privacy
Your material is linked only to your account. Another mentor's student assistant doesn't use your documents.
:::

## Reference

| Format | Maximum size per file |
| --- | --- |
| PDF | 20 MB |
| Word (DOCX) | 10 MB |
| TXT or MD | 5 MB |

At the top of the section, the counters show how many **Documents** you've uploaded (out of the total allowed) and the **Storage** used.

| Document status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending** / **Processing** | The file arrived and is being read. Wait a few seconds |
| **Ready** | The assistant already uses this material |
| **Error** | The file couldn't be read. Try **Reprocess** |

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>It said "Unsupported file type".</summary>

Only PDF, DOCX, TXT, or MD work. If your file is another type (for example, a spreadsheet or a presentation), save it as a PDF and upload it again.

</details>

<details>
<summary>It said "Document limit reached" or "Storage limit reached".</summary>

You've reached your account's maximum. Remove a document you no longer use, with the trash icon, and upload the new one.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I updated my workbook. What do I do?</summary>

Remove the old version and upload the new one. That way the assistant doesn't mix the two.

</details>

<details>
<summary>The document is stuck on Error.</summary>

Click **Reprocess**. If it stays on **Error**, remove the document and upload the file again.

</details>
