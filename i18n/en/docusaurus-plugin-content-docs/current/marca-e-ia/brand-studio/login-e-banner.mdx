In the **Login & Conteúdo** (Login & Content) step of Brand Studio, you set the first screen the student sees, where the menu goes, and the banner at the top of the home page.

<Aula slug="brand-studio-p2-login" />

## Step by step

<Passos>
  <Passo titulo={<>In the <strong>Área do Aluno</strong> (Student Area) editor, open the <strong>Login & Conteúdo</strong> step.</>} />
  <Passo titulo={<>In <strong>Modelo de Layout</strong> (Layout Template), choose how the login screen looks.</>}>
    Centered, with a banner, or with a video on the side.

    ![Login screen templates](/img/guia/brand-studio-p2-login/02-layout.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>In <strong>Navegação da área do aluno</strong> (Student area navigation), choose <strong>Barra no topo</strong> (Top bar) or <strong>Menu lateral</strong> (Side menu).</>}>
    The side menu looks like a streaming app.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>In the <strong>Banner Hero</strong>, write the <strong>Título</strong> (Title) and the <strong>Descrição</strong> (Description).</>}>
    It's the highlight at the top of the home page.

    ![Banner Hero fields](/img/guia/brand-studio-p2-login/05-banner.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Click <strong>Salvar alterações</strong> (Save changes).</>}>
    In a few minutes, students see the new area.
  </Passo>
</Passos>

## Good to know

:::tip
Want several highlights at the top? Turn on **Múltiplos banners de destaque** (Multiple highlight banners): up to 4 images rotate on their own, 12 seconds each.
:::

:::caution
Saving changes what students see right away. Use the preview on the right to check first.
:::

## Reference

| Option | Choices |
| --- | --- |
| **Modelo de Layout** (login) | **Centralizado** (Centered), **À esquerda** (Left), **À direita** (Right), **Banner Direito** (Banner right), **Banner Esquerdo** (Banner left), **Vídeo Direito** (Video right) |
| **Navegação da área do aluno** | **Barra no topo** or **Menu lateral** |
| **Imagem do Banner** (Banner image) | PNG, JPG, or WebP |

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>I don't see the change in the student area.</summary>

It can take a few minutes. If a notice says the Showcase didn't update, your changes are saved: try saving again in a few minutes.

</details>

<summary>Where do I change the colors and the logo?</summary>

In the **Identidade** (Identity) step. See [Give the student area your brand](/docs/marca-e-ia/brand-studio/sua-marca).

</details>
