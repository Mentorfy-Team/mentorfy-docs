Brand Studio makes the student area look like your brand: colors, logo, and layout, no code needed. Use it when you set up the student area for the first time or want to refresh its look.

<Aula slug="brand-studio" />

## Step by step

<Passos>
  <Passo titulo={<>In the menu, click <strong>Brand Studio</strong>.</>}>
    It opens in a new tab and you're already logged in.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>On the <strong>Área do Aluno</strong> (Student Area) card, click <strong>Editar</strong> (Edit).</>}>
    On the right, the preview shows what your students will see.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>In the <strong>Identidade</strong> (Identity) step, pick a ready-made palette.</>}>
    For example, **Pôr do Sol** (Sunset). The preview changes right away.

    ![Ready-made palettes in the Identidade step](/img/guia/brand-studio/03-paletas.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Under <strong>Logo</strong>, upload your file or click <strong>Gerar com IA</strong> (Generate with AI).</>}>
    The AI already uses the palette's colors.

    ![Generate image with AI window](/img/guia/brand-studio/06-logo-ia.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Click <strong>Gerar</strong> (Generate) and, when the logo is ready, <strong>Escolher esta imagem</strong> (Choose this image).</>} />
  <Passo titulo={<>Open the <strong>Revisão</strong> (Review) step, check everything, and click <strong>Salvar Alterações</strong> (Save Changes).</>}>
    In a few minutes, your students see the new brand.
  </Passo>
</Passos>

## Good to know

:::caution Saving applies to students right away
When you click **Salvar Alterações**, students start seeing the changes. Use the preview to check first. Colors and images can take a few minutes to show up.
:::

:::tip
Like a color combination? Use **Salvar par como preset** (Save pair as preset) to reuse it later. It's stored in this browser.
:::

## Reference

The 7 steps of the **Área do Aluno** editor:

| Step | What you set |
| --- | --- |
| **Identidade** (Identity) | Name, colors, logo, and language |
| **Login & Conteúdo** (Login & Content) | Login screen, menu, and banner |
| **SEO** | How the area shows up in searches and in shared links |
| **Funcionalidades** (Features) | Features and access |
| **Domínio** (Domain) | Custom domain |
| **Categorias** (Categories) | How products are organized |
| **Revisão** (Review) | Check everything and save |

Ready-made palettes: **Padrão** (Default), **Oceano** (Ocean), **Floresta** (Forest), **Royal**, and **Pôr do Sol** (Sunset). The logo accepts PNG, JPG, or WebP.

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>I clicked Brand Studio and another tab opened. Is that right?</summary>

Yes. Brand Studio is a separate site, linked to your account. It opens in a new tab and logs you in automatically. To go back to the dashboard, go back to the previous tab.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I saved, but the student still sees the old colors.</summary>

Colors and images can take a few minutes to show up. Wait a bit and reload the student area page.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I have more than one student area?</summary>

No. Each account has only one **Área do Aluno**. You edit the same area whenever you want.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Someone on my team can't find Brand Studio.</summary>

That person needs permission to view Brand Studio. Without it, the dashboard sends them back to the home page. Adjust permissions in [My team](/docs/grupos-e-equipe/meu-time).

</details>
