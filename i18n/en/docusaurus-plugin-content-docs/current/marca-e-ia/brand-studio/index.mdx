**Brand Studio makes the student area look like your brand.**

- Pick a ready-made color palette or create your own.
- Upload your logo or create one with AI.
- Choose the login screen, the menu, and the home page banner.

:::info Opens in another tab
Brand Studio is a separate site, linked to your account. When you click **Brand Studio** in the menu, it opens in a new tab and you're already logged in, with no password to type. To go back to the dashboard, just go back to the previous tab.
:::
