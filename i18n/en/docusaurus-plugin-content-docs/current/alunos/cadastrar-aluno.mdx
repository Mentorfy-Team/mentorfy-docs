Use this when someone joined outside your sales platforms (a Pix payment, a free spot, a guest). The student gets an email with their access and finds the mentorship waiting in their area.

On the Mentorfy screen, a student shows up as a **client**, in the **Clients** menu.

<Aula slug="cadastrar-aluno" />

## Step by step

<Passos>
  <Passo titulo={<>In the menu, open <strong>Clients</strong> › <strong>My Clients</strong> and click <strong>Register</strong>.</>}>
    It's the orange button at the end of the row of buttons, above the list.

    ![My Clients screen with the Register button on the right](/img/guia/cadastrar-aluno/botoes.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Fill in <strong>Email</strong> and <strong>Name</strong>.</>}>
    These are the only required fields. Phone, tags, and **Access Expiration** are optional.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Leave <strong>Notify user by email</strong> turned on.</>}>
    When it's on, the student gets the email with their access. When it's off, they get access but no notice at all.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>In <strong>Select the track to assign</strong>, pick a track if you want.</>}>
    A track gives access to all of its mentorships at once.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>In <strong>Select the product to assign</strong>, pick the mentorship.</>}>
    You can pick more than one. You need to pick at least one track or one product.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Click <strong>Create Client</strong>.</>}>
    A green notice confirms it, and the student shows up in the list.

    ![Filled-in Register Client form, with the Create Client button](/img/guia/cadastrar-aluno/formulario.jpg)
  </Passo>
</Passos>

## Good to know

:::tip Want to see what the student sees?
In the list, click the three dots on the student's row and choose **Log in as Student**. You enter their area without needing their password.
:::

:::info Was the student already registered?
You can register them again with the same email. Nobody gets duplicated: they just get the new products. If they already had the product, nothing changes.
:::

## Reference

| Field | Required? | What it's for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Email** | Yes | It's the student's login. The access email goes here. |
| **Name** | Yes | Shows up in the list and in the welcome email. |
| **Phone (Optional)** | No | With area code. Shows up in the **Contact** column of the list. |
| **Tags** | No | Labels to sort your students (e.g., "VIP"). |
| **Access Expiration (optional)** | No | The date the access ends. Empty = no end date. |
| **Notify user by email** | Already on | Decides whether the student gets the access email. |
| **Select the track / the product to assign** | At least one of the two | What the student will be able to access. |

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>What arrives in the student's email?</summary>

A new student gets a welcome email with the link to your area and the password, or a button to create a password. Someone who already had an account gets a notice about the new products, and their password doesn't change.

</details>

<details>
<summary>A notice said the access email wasn't sent. What now?</summary>

The registration worked; only the email failed. To send it again, click the three dots on the student's row and choose **Send access email**. Their password doesn't change. See more causes in [My student didn't get her access](/docs/alunos/aluna-sem-acesso).

</details>

<details>
<summary>The Register button is grayed out. Why?</summary>

There are two possible causes: your account has no active Mentorfy plan, or you're part of a team and don't have permission to create clients. In that case, ask the account owner to give you access.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I don't see the "Request password creation on first access" or "Register Guardian" options.</summary>

They only show up when your student area is set up for them: password creation on first access and guardian registration (parents or tutors). Without those settings, the form is shorter.

</details>
