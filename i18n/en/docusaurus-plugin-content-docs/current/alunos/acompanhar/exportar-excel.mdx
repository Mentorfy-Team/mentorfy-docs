Need to send your students' progress to a corporate client or your team? Download a ready-made spreadsheet with each student's progress.

<Aula slug="acompanhamento-p2-excel" />

## Step by step

<Passos>
  <Passo titulo={<>In <strong>Journey</strong> › <strong>Tracking</strong>, click <strong>Export Excel</strong>.</>} />
  <Passo titulo={<>If you want, pick a group in <strong>Students' group</strong>.</>}>
    For example, the employees of a company. The spreadsheet lists only the students in that group.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Pick the <strong>Format</strong>.</>}>
    **One spreadsheet per product**: one tab for each product, with the numbers and the list of students. **Everything in one tab**: all students in a single tab, easy to filter in Excel.

    ![Export journey (Excel) window with group, format, and products](/img/guia/acompanhamento-p2-excel/exportar.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Check the products and click <strong>Download Excel</strong>.</>}>
    To check them all, use **Select all**. The file downloads to your computer.
  </Passo>
</Passos>

## Good to know

:::tip A report for a corporate client
Put the company's employees in a group and pick that group when exporting. The product numbers then count only that group, too.
:::

## Reference

| Format | What you get | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **One spreadsheet per product** | One tab per product: access, participating, average progress, and the student table | Up to 80 products |
| **Everything in one tab** | A "Students" tab with the product, the numbers, and each student's data per row | No such limit |

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>It says I selected too many products.</summary>

**One spreadsheet per product** accepts up to 80 products. Remove some from the selection or switch to **Everything in one tab**.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I can't find a product in the list.</summary>

When you have a lot of products, the list shows only some of them. Check the **Include all … products** option, which shows above the list.

</details>
