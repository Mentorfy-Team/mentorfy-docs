**The Overview shows the health of your whole mentorship; Tracking shows each student, product by product.** Both are in the **Journey** menu.

Here you learn how to:

- see in one minute whether your students are active, making progress, and finishing;
- see who got stuck on which module and open each student's progress;
- export progress to Excel for a corporate client or your team.

In groups, your team also tracks contact with each client. See [What counts as contact](/docs/alunos/acompanhar/o-que-conta-como-contato).
