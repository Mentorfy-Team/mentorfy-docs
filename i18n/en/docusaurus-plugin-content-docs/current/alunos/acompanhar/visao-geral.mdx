In the **Overview**, you see in one minute whether your students are active, making progress, and finishing. You also find out the time of day they study most.

<Aula slug="visao-geral" />

## Step by step

<Passos>
  <Passo titulo={<>In the menu, open <strong>Journey</strong> › <strong>Overview</strong>.</>} />
  <Passo titulo={<>Pick the period: <strong>7 days</strong>, <strong>30 days</strong>, <strong>90 days</strong>, or <strong>1 year</strong>.</>} />
  <Passo titulo={<>Read the four cards: <strong>Students</strong>, <strong>Interactions</strong>, <strong>Average Progress</strong>, and <strong>Completions</strong>.</>}>
    Under each number, the comparison with the previous period shows whether it got better. What each number means is in the table below.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>In the <strong>Activity Trend</strong> chart, see whether activity is going up or down.</>} />
  <Passo titulo={<>In the <strong>Activity by Time</strong> map, find the busiest time.</>}>
    The stronger the color, the more students are studying. Use that time to send your notices.

    ![Activity by Time heat map and, below it, Top Students and Top Products](/img/guia/visao-geral/mapa-de-calor.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Further down, see <strong>Top Students</strong> and <strong>Top Products</strong>.</>}>
    These are the students and products making the most progress in the period.
  </Passo>
</Passos>

## Good to know

:::tip Use the best time
Found the busy time on the map? Schedule your next notice for it in [Schedule a send](/docs/alunos/notificacoes/agendar-envio).
:::

## Reference

| Card | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Students** | **Active**: students who interacted at least once in the period. **Total**: students enrolled in any of your products, all time. |
| **Interactions** | Answers and submissions in the steps, made in the period. |
| **Average Progress** | Average progress of those taking part: completed steps divided by the total steps in the product. |
| **Completions** | Students who completed at least 90% of a product (all-time total). "New" ones are those that passed 90% within the period. |

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>The numbers are low for 7 or 30 days. Is something wrong?</summary>

Not necessarily. With few students or little recent activity, pick **90 days** or **1 year** to see the trend with more data.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I see a specific student?</summary>

The Overview shows the big picture. To see student by student, use [Tracking](/docs/alunos/acompanhar/acompanhamento).

</details>
