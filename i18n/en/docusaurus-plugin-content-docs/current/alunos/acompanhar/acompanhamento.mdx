In **Tracking**, you see who is moving forward, who got stuck, and who needs a nudge. All product by product, down to each student's details.

<Aula slug="acompanhamento" />

## Step by step

<Passos>
  <Passo titulo={<>In the menu, open <strong>Journey</strong> › <strong>Tracking</strong> and pick the product. Click <strong>View Details</strong>.</>}>
    Each card shows **Access** (how many have access), **Participating** (how many have already interacted with some step), and **Average Progress**.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>In the <strong>Funnel</strong> tab, see how many students reached each module.</>}>
    ![Funnel tab with a product's Module Progression](/img/guia/acompanhamento/funil.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>In the <strong>Kanban</strong> tab, see each student in the module they're on.</>} />
  <Passo titulo={<>In the <strong>Students</strong> tab, see each one's progress and last activity.</>}>
    The **Progress** and **Last Activity** columns show who stopped.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Click a student to see each step they did.</>}>
    If the product has essays, the **Essays** block shows up at the top of the panel. Click **Analyze** to grade them.
  </Passo>
</Passos>

## Good to know

:::tip Filter by group
At the top of the screen, switch **All groups** to one group to see only those students. Useful for tracking a company or a class.
:::

:::tip Reach out to those who got stuck
See students who stopped? Click **Notify** to send a notice to the students of this product. See [Notify your students](/docs/alunos/notificacoes/avisar-alunos).
:::

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>I want to see one student across all products, not one product at a time.</summary>

On the Tracking home screen, switch the view from **Products** to **Students** and click the student. You see their progress in each product.

</details>

<details>
<summary>The numbers look out of date.</summary>

The data is refreshed from time to time; the time until the next refresh shows under the title. To refresh right away, click the **Refresh data now** button (the little arrows in a circle).

</details>

<details>
<summary>What's the difference between Access and Participating?</summary>

**Access** counts who can enter the product. **Participating** counts who has already interacted with at least one of its steps. The difference between the two is the students who haven't started yet.

</details>
