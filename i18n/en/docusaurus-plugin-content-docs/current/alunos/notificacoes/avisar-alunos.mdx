Remind your students about a live class, a deadline, or new content, on the channel they read most. Each student's name goes into the message on its own.

<Aula slug="notificacoes" />

## Step by step

<Passos>
  <Passo titulo={<>In the menu, open <strong>Journey</strong> › <strong>Notifications</strong> and click <strong>Notify students</strong>.</>}>
    This screen also has the **Send History**, with everything that has been sent.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Pick the channel and click <strong>Compose message</strong>.</>}>
    You can check more than one channel. Email is unlimited; SMS and phone calls use credits.

    ![Select channels step, with Email, WhatsApp, SMS, and Phone Call](/img/guia/notificacoes/canais.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Write the <strong>Subject</strong> and the <strong>Message</strong>.</>}>
    In **Insert variable**, choose **Student name**: each student gets the message with their own name.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Choose who gets it and click <strong>Review</strong>.</>}>
    Filter by product or group and check the students. You can also paste a list of emails in **Add by email**.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Check everything in the review and click <strong>Send message</strong>.</>}>
    At the end, the screen shows how many people the message was sent to.
  </Passo>
</Passos>

## Good to know

:::danger Once it's sent, it's gone
When you click **Send message**, the notices go out right away and can't be undone. Check the text and the recipients first.
:::

:::info Every channel goes through Agendafy
To send on any channel, your account needs to be linked to Agendafy. Without that, the window shows **Connect your Agendafy account**, with the **Connect with Agendafy** button, which takes you to Integrations.
:::

## Reference

| Channel | Cost | What it needs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Email** | Unlimited | Account linked to Agendafy |
| **WhatsApp** | — | WhatsApp connected in **Integrations** |
| **SMS** | Credits: every 160 characters = 1 credit, per student | SMS credits |
| **Phone Call** | Credits per student | Phone call credits. The text is read out loud. |
| **Push (App)** | — | Coming soon, only for White-Label apps |

Your balance shows at the top of the window, in **Credits**. To buy more, click **Reload**.

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>SMS and Phone Call are grayed out.</summary>

You're out of credits: the window shows "Insufficient credits to use this channel." Click **Reload** to buy more.

</details>

<details>
<summary>WhatsApp is grayed out.</summary>

Your account's WhatsApp isn't connected or hasn't been set up yet. Connect it in **Integrations**. See [Integrations](/docs/integracoes/intro).

</details>

<details>
<summary>I pasted emails, but some didn't get added.</summary>

Only emails of people registered as your students get added. The others show up in **Not found** and don't get the message.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I know it arrived?</summary>

Go back to **Notifications** and click the send in the **Send History**. See [Schedule and check the send](/docs/alunos/notificacoes/agendar-envio).

</details>
