Schedule the notice for the time your students study most, and then check in the history that it arrived.

<Aula slug="notificacoes-p2-agendar" />

## Step by step

<Passos>
  <Passo titulo={<>In <strong>Notify students</strong>, pick the channel and turn on <strong>Schedule send</strong>.</>}>
    When you turn it on, the date is already set for tomorrow at 8 a.m. The time zone being used shows below the title.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Use a shortcut (<strong>Tomorrow</strong>, <strong>In 2 days</strong>, <strong>Next week</strong>) or click the date and pick the day and time.</>}>
    ![Schedule send turned on, with the shortcuts and the chosen date](/img/guia/notificacoes-p2-agendar/agendar.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Write the message and pick the students.</>}>
    You can filter by product or by group. Same as [sending right away](/docs/alunos/notificacoes/avisar-alunos).
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>In the review, check the time in <strong>Scheduled for</strong> and confirm.</>}>
    The send is scheduled and shows up on the calendar on the Notifications screen.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>After it goes out, click the item in the <strong>Send History</strong>.</>}>
    In **Send Details**, you see the status, the channels, who got it, and the time of each step.
  </Passo>
</Passos>

## Good to know

:::tip What's the best time?
Look at the **Activity by Time** map in the [Overview](/docs/alunos/acompanhar/visao-geral) and schedule for the busiest time.
:::

:::caution Check before you confirm
There's no button on this screen to cancel a send that's already scheduled. Check the date, the text, and the recipients before you confirm.
:::

## Reference

| Status in the history | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Scheduled** | Scheduled, hasn't gone out yet. |
| **Pending** or **Retrying** | It's going out, or trying again after a failure. |
| **Success** | Sent. |
| **Partial** | Went out to only some of the recipients. |
| **Failed** | Didn't go out. Open the details to see the error. |

At the top of the screen, the **Sent**, **Scheduled**, **Success Rate**, and **Failures** cards sum it all up.

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>What shows up in Send Details?</summary>

The status, the channels used, the list of recipients, the timeline (**Created at**, **Scheduled for**, **Processed at**), and the content of the message.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I find an old send?</summary>

In the **Send History**, use **Filter by status**. The calendar also shows each day's sends, colored by status.

</details>
