In **Messages**, students ask for help and your team answers in a queue. Each conversation has an owner, so nobody answers twice. You can also message a student first.

On the Mentorfy screen, a student shows up as a **client**, in the **Clients** menu.

<Aula slug="mensagens" />

## Step by step

<Passos>
  <Passo titulo={<>In the menu, open <strong>Clients</strong> › <strong>Messages</strong>.</>}>
    Conversations sit in the **Mine**, **Unassigned**, **Urgent**, **All**, and **Resolved** queues. Whoever has waited the longest comes first.

    ![Messages inbox with the queues and the New message button](/img/guia/mensagens/central.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>To reach out to a student, click <strong>New message</strong> and search by name or email.</>} />
  <Passo titulo={<>Write the subject and the message and click <strong>Send</strong>.</>}>
    The subject is optional. You become the owner of the conversation.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>In the conversation, see everything about the student on the right.</>}>
    **About the student** shows their products, group, and past conversations.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>To leave a note for your team, open the <strong>Internal note</strong> tab and click <strong>Save note</strong>.</>}>
    The student doesn't see the note.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>When you're done, click <strong>Resolve</strong>.</>}>
    The conversation leaves the queue and goes to **Resolved**.
  </Passo>
</Passos>

## Good to know

:::tip A conversation without an owner
In the **Unassigned** queue, click **Take it** to make the conversation yours. That way the rest of the team knows someone is already on it.
:::

:::info The student finds out you replied
If the student doesn't have the app open, they get an email notice when you reply.
:::

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>My students can't find where to send a message.</summary>

The button only shows up for them when **Messages** is turned on in the student app. Turn it on in **Support settings** › **In the student app** › **Show "Messages" in the student area menu**. See [Set up your support](/docs/alunos/mensagens/configurar-atendimento).

</details>

<details>
<summary>What's the difference between Send and Send and resolve?</summary>

**Send** sends the reply and the conversation waits for the student. **Send and resolve** sends it, closes the conversation, and asks the student to rate the support.

</details>

<details>
<summary>The student replied to a resolved conversation. What now?</summary>

The conversation reopens on its own and goes back to the queue. You can also reopen a conversation with **Reopen**.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I send files?</summary>

Yes, with the **Attach** button. You can send up to 10 files per message.

</details>
