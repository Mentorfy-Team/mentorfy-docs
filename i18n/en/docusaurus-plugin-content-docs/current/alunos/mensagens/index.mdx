**Messages is your support inbox: students ask for help from the app, and your team answers in a queue, with nobody answering twice.**

Here you learn how to:

- answer, leave internal notes for your team, and resolve conversations;
- set your hours, reply target, who answers, and the topics;
- reply in one click and let AI handle the common questions.
