In **Support settings**, you decide when your team is available, how fast you promise to reply, and who looks after conversations. Everything the student sees about hours and reply time comes from here.

On the Mentorfy screen, a student shows up as a **client**, in the **Clients** menu.

<Aula slug="mensagens-p2-configurar" />

## Step by step

<Passos>
  <Passo titulo={<>In <strong>Messages</strong>, click the <strong>Support settings</strong> icon in the top corner.</>}>
    It's the settings icon, next to the question mark.

    ![Support settings open on the Hours and target section](/img/guia/mensagens-p2-configurar/configuracoes.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>In <strong>Hours and target</strong>, check your support days and hours.</>}>
    Outside those hours, the student gets an automatic notice. You can change the text of that notice.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Pick the <strong>First response target</strong>.</>}>
    The student sees this promise ("we reply within 4 hours"). In the queue, the conversation turns yellow near the limit and red after it.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>In <strong>Team and alerts</strong>, turn on who on your team answers and decide when to alert them.</>}>
    In **Alert the assignee when a conversation waits more than**, pick the waiting time that triggers the alert.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>In <strong>Topics</strong>, give <strong>High</strong> priority to what's urgent.</>}>
    For example, "Access and password". Priority decides who shows up first in the queue.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Click <strong>Save changes</strong>.</>} />
</Passos>

## Good to know

:::tip Who gets each new conversation
The conversation goes to the person in charge of the student in **My Clients**. If the student has no one in charge, it goes into the **Unassigned** queue.
:::

:::info Priority applies to new conversations
Changing a topic's priority affects upcoming conversations. For the ones already open, change them one by one, inside the conversation.
:::

## Reference

How support comes out of the box, before you change anything:

| Setting | Default | Options |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Days and hours | Monday to Friday, 9 a.m. to 6 p.m. (Brasília time) | Any day and time |
| **First response target** | 4 hours | 30 minutes, 1, 2, 4, or 8 hours, 1 or 2 days |
| **Alert the assignee when a conversation waits more than** | 30 minutes | 15 or 30 minutes, 1, 2, or 4 hours |
| **Topics** | Lesson question, Access and password, Billing, Scheduling, Other topic | Up to 20 topics |
| Out-of-hours notice and automatic confirmation | On | Turn on or off, and change the text |
| **Students can start conversations** | On | When off, only your team starts conversations |
| **Ask for a rating when a conversation is resolved** | On | Turn on or off |

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>My students don't see the Messages button in the app.</summary>

In the **In the student app** section, turn on **Show "Messages" in the student area menu**. It's the same setting as in Brand Studio. If your student area hasn't been published in Brand Studio yet, publish it first.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I want only my team to start conversations.</summary>

In **In the student app**, turn off **Students can start conversations**. Students can still reply to conversations your team opens with the **New message** button.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I closed it without saving. Did I lose my changes?</summary>

Yes. Changes only take effect after **Save changes**. At the bottom, **Unsaved changes** tells you when something still needs saving; **All saved** confirms it.

</details>
