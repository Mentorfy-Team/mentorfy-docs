Two helpers so your team repeats itself less: **quick replies**, which go into the conversation in one click, and **Quick Assist**, where AI answers the common questions before your team does.

<Aula slug="mensagens-p3-respostas" />

## Step by step

<Passos>
  <Passo titulo={<>In <strong>Support settings</strong>, open <strong>Quick replies</strong> and click <strong>New reply</strong>.</>} />
  <Passo titulo={<>Give it a <strong>Title</strong> and a <strong>Shortcut</strong>, and write the text. Click <strong>Save</strong>.</>}>
    Write `{nome}` where the student's name should go and `{mentor}` for yours.

    ![New quick reply with title, shortcut, and text](/img/guia/mensagens-p3-respostas/nova-resposta.jpg)
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>In the conversation, type <strong>/</strong> and pick the reply.</>}>
    It goes in ready, with the student's name filled in.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>In <strong>Quick Assist</strong>, click <strong>New question</strong> and add the questions you get most often.</>}>
    For each one, write the **Question** and the **Answer** and click **Save**. Have a ready-made list? Use **Import**.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Turn on <strong>Quick Assist</strong> and pick the <strong>Tries before calling the team</strong>.</>}>
    It only turns on after you add at least one question.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Click <strong>Save changes</strong>.</>}>
    From then on, AI tries to solve it. If it can't, it hands the conversation to your team.
  </Passo>
</Passos>

## Good to know

:::caution Quick Assist uses AI credits
Each AI answer uses 0.35 AI credit from your account. With no credits, the conversation goes straight to your team. See your credits in [Plan and credits](/docs/conta/plano-e-creditos).
:::

:::info AI only answers what you added
It uses your questions and answers and doesn't make up deadlines, prices, links, or passwords. Money, cancellations, complaints, or an upset student always go to your team.
:::

## Reference

| Setting | Default | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Quick Assist** | Off | Needs at least 1 question |
| **Tries before calling the team** | 3 | From 1 to 5 |
| **Frequently asked questions** | None | Up to 50 |
| **Also use the copilot material** | Off | Also searches the Student Copilot material |

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>AI is answering and I want to take over. How?</summary>

In the conversation, click **Take over**. Quick Assist stops there, and the conversation is yours.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can the student ask to talk to a person?</summary>

Yes. When they ask, AI hands the conversation to your team and lets the student know.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I import many questions at once?</summary>

In **Quick Assist**, click **Import**. Paste the list (one question per line, with the answer next to it) or attach a .csv or .xlsx spreadsheet with the "pergunta" (question) and "resposta" (answer) columns. You check everything before saving.

</details>
