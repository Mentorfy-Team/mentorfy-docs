In **Comments**, you see everything students write in the lessons, across all your courses, and you answer without opening each lesson.

:::info Only for Online Course products
Comments only exist in **Online Course** products, and they start **turned off**. Turn them on product by product (step 2). The screen has the **Experimental** badge: it may still change.
:::

On the Mentorfy screen, a student shows up as a **client**, in the **Clients** menu.

## Step by step

<Passos>
  <Passo titulo={<>In the menu, open <strong>Clients</strong> › <strong>Comments</strong>.</>}>
    At the top, the **Total**, **Unread**, and **New Today** cards sum things up.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Open <strong>Comments Configuration by Product</strong> and turn on comments for the course.</>}>
    On the same row, pick the **Visibility** and whether students can reply to each other (**Student Replies**).
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Find the comment in the list.</>}>
    Filter by product, by **Status** (**Unread** or **Read**), and by **Period**, or search by student or text.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Click the eye icon (<strong>View details</strong>).</>}>
    It opens the full comment, the lesson, the product, and any replies that already exist.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>In <strong>Reply</strong>, write your answer and click <strong>Send reply</strong>.</>}>
    The **Reply sent successfully** notice confirms it.
  </Passo>
</Passos>

## Good to know

:::tip Clear your unread queue
Read everything? Click **Mark all as read**. For just one, use the **Mark as read** icon on the row.
:::

:::danger Deleting a comment can't be undone
With **Delete comment**, the comment and all its replies are gone for good.
:::

## Reference

| Per-product setting | Options | What changes for the student |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Comments** | On or off | When off, the lesson doesn't show comments. It starts off. |
| **Visibility** | **Public** or **Private** | Public: all students see the comments. Private: each student sees only their own. You see all of them. |
| **Student Replies** | On or off | When on, students reply to each other. When off, only you and your team reply. |

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>My course doesn't show up in the configuration.</summary>

Only **Online Course** products show up. Mentorships of other types don't have comments in their lessons.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I'm on the team. Can I reply as the mentor?</summary>

Yes. For team members, the window shows a switch above the reply: off, you reply with your own name; on, the reply goes out with the name of the mentor who owns the account.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Where does the student see my reply?</summary>

In the lesson itself, next to the comment they made. They also get a notification saying there's a new reply to their comment, with a direct link to the lesson.

</details>
