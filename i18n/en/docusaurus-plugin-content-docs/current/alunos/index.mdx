**A student is a client who bought or was given access to one of your mentorships.** On screen, they show up as a "client", under **Clients** › **My Clients**.

Here you learn how to:

- bring students in, one at a time or the whole class at once;
- give, renew, or remove access for many students at once;
- support, track, and notify your students.
