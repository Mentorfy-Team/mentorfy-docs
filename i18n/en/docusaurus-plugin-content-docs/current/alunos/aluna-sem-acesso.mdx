Go through the list from top to bottom. Each item tells you **how to check** and **how to fix**. The problem is almost always in the first three.

:::tip Start here
In **Clients** › **My Clients**, search for the student by name or email. If she **doesn't show up**, skip straight to item 5. If she does, click the three dots on her row and choose **Log in as Student**: you see exactly what she sees.
:::

On the Mentorfy screen, a student shows up as a **client**, in the **Clients** menu.

## 1. The access email wasn't sent or didn't arrive

**How to check:** look at the student's **Email Status** column.

- **Delivered**, **Opened**, or **Clicked**: the email arrived. Go to item 2.
- **Bounced**: the address doesn't exist or is blocked. Go to item 3.
- **Deferred**: her email provider is refusing it for now; it will try sending again on its own.
- **No info**: there's no email record for her. Maybe no email was sent, which happens when she was registered with **Notify user by email** turned off, or through [bulk access](/docs/alunos/acesso-em-massa), which doesn't send emails.

**How to fix:**

<Passos>
  <Passo titulo={<>Click the three dots on the student's row.</>} />
  <Passo titulo={<>Choose <strong>Send access email</strong>.</>}>
    Her password doesn't change. The email can take 1 to 3 minutes to arrive.
  </Passo>
</Passos>

## 2. The email went to spam

**How to check:** the **Email Status** column shows **Spam**, or it shows **Delivered** and she says she can't find anything.

**How to fix:** ask her to look in the **Spam**, **Junk**, and **Promotions** folders, mark the email as "not spam", and click the link. Still can't find it? Resend it as in item 1.

## 3. The email address was typed wrong

**How to check:** compare the email in the list, letter by letter, with the one she uses. Common mistakes: `gmail.con`, `hotmial.com`, an extra dot.

**How to fix:**

<Passos>
  <Passo titulo={<>Click <strong>Register</strong> and register the student with the right email and the same product.</>}>
    See [Register a student](/docs/alunos/cadastrar-aluno).
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>If you want, delete the wrong registration: three dots › <strong>Remove Client</strong>.</>} />
</Passos>

:::danger Remove Client deletes everything
Removing a client deletes their whole history, and it can't be undone. Only remove the registration made with the wrong email.
:::

## 4. The product isn't released or has expired

**How to check:** in the **Products** column, see whether the mentorship shows up for her and whether it's **Active** or **Expired**. In her area, the mentorship shows as **ACCESS EXPIRED** or **NOT RELEASED**.

**How to fix:**

- **She doesn't have the product:** three dots › **Add Product**.
- **It expired:** use [bulk access](/docs/alunos/acesso-em-massa) with **Lifetime access**, **Extend expiration**, or **Specific date**. The **Keep current expiration** option doesn't renew anyone who has already expired.

## 5. The access is waiting for your approval

**How to check:** in the menu, open **Clients** › **Approvals**. There you'll find students who asked for access from the student area (the **REQUEST ACCESS** button) and purchases whose payment is still pending. For the student, the mentorship shows as **AWAITING APPROVAL**.

**How to fix:** click **Approve Access** on her row. **Reject Access** deletes the request.

## 6. The purchase from an integration didn't come through

**How to check:** the student bought through Hotmart, Kiwify, or another platform, but she doesn't show up in **My Clients**. If she does show up, click the three dots › **View Profile** and open **View payment events** to see what the platform sent.

**How to fix:** check that the sales platform is connected and that the product sold is linked to the right mentorship. The step-by-step for each platform is in [Integrations](/docs/integracoes/intro). Meanwhile, you can [register the student](/docs/alunos/cadastrar-aluno) by hand.

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>Does resending the access change the student's password?</summary>

No. **Send access email** sends the access details again, and the password stays the same. If she forgot her password, use **Generate new password**, in the same three-dots menu.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Is it a problem to register the student again with the same email?</summary>

No. She isn't duplicated: she just gets the new products. With **Notify user by email** turned on, she gets an email letting her know.

</details>
