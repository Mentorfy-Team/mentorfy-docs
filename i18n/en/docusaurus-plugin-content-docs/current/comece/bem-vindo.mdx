Mentorfy is where you build, sell, and track your mentorships, all in one place.
You create the lessons, add your students, and organize your team to take care of each one.
AI helps along the way: it creates covers and audio, answers questions, and analyzes your numbers.

## How this guide works

Each page teaches **one task**. On almost every page you will find:

- **A short video**, under one minute, showing the real screen.
- **The step by step** in text, to follow at your own pace.
- **The Open in Mentorfy button**, below the video. It opens the right screen in a new tab.

:::tip Tip
Keep the guide on one side and Mentorfy on the other. That way you do it together, step by step.
:::
