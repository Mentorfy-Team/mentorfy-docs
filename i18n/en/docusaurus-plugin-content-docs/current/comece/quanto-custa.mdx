In Mentorfy you pay for three things: the **plan**, the **add-ons** you choose to add to it and, if you use a lot of AI, more **AI credits**.

## Plan

The plan is your main subscription. It sets how many **clients**, **mentorships**, and **teams** you can have, and which features are included. The plans are **Fuji**, **Olympus**, and **Everest**.

During the free trial, you use the **trial plan** for 21 days.

## Add-ons

Add-ons are extras you add to your plan and cancel whenever you want.

| Add-on | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **AI Credits Subscription** | More AI credits, renewed every month. |
| **Meeting** | Live meeting rooms, with recording. |
| **Extra Storage** | More space for videos and materials. |
| **Extra Mentoring** | Create more mentorships beyond your plan's limit. |
| **Community** | A space to bring your students together, with a feed, groups, and conversations. |

## AI credits

Credits are the "currency" of the AI tools. Each time the AI creates something for you, it uses some credits.

- **Monthly:** the credits that come with your plan or with the **AI Credits Subscription**. They renew every month and **don't roll over** from one month to the next.
- **One-off:** extra credits, like the free trial welcome bonus.
- The platform uses the monthly credits first, then the one-off ones.

:::info During the free trial
You get **50 welcome credits**. They are valid until the end of the trial. After that, they disappear.
:::

### What uses credits

| Where | What | How much |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI Studio | Create an image (e.g., a course cover) | 5 credits |
| AI Studio | Narration (text to speech) | 1 credit per 1,000 characters |
| AI Studio | Podcast (conversation between narrators) | 2 credits per 1,000 characters (minimum of 2) |
| AI Studio | **Voice to Voice** (change the voice in an audio) | 3 credits |
| AI Studio | Create a song | 5 credits |
| Storage | **Dubbing** a video | 10 credits per minute, per language |
| My Copilot | Each message | 0.5 credit |
| Student Copilot | Each answer to a student | 0.35 credit |
| Messages | Each **Quick Assist** answer | 0.35 credit |

:::tip Copilots on paid plans
Today, conversations with **My Copilot**, the **Student Copilot**, and **Quick Assist** only use credits during the free trial. On paid plans, they don't come out of your balance. But the Student Copilot only turns on if you have credits available.
:::

If an AI Studio image fails, the credit comes back automatically.

## Where to see your balance

In **My Account**, on the **Subscription** tab, in the **AI Credits** row. See the step by step in [See your plan and your credits](/docs/conta/plano-e-creditos).

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>How much do more credits cost?</summary>

In the **AI Credits Subscription**, the monthly packages shown on screen are: 250 credits (R$ 49.00/month), 1,000 (R$ 174.00/month), 5,000 (R$ 874.00/month), and 10,000 (R$ 1,747.00/month). Always check the price on screen before subscribing.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What about more space for videos?</summary>

In **Extra Storage**, the monthly packages shown on screen are: 100 GB (R$ 30.00/month), 250 GB (R$ 75.00/month), and 500 GB (R$ 150.00/month). To change or cancel this add-on, talk to the sales team.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Are meetings included in the free trial?</summary>

Yes. During the trial, you can hold meetings with up to 10 participants, in 1 room. After the trial, meetings depend on the **Meeting** add-on. Some plans already include it.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What happens if I run out of credits?</summary>

The tools that use credits won't create anything until you have a balance again. To get more, subscribe to credits in **My Account**, using the **Subscribe more** button in the **AI Credits** row.

</details>
