The free trial lasts **21 days**. To make the most of it, Mentorfy shows the **Free trial roadmap**: 6 short missions that cover the essentials of the platform.

## Where to find the roadmap

The **Free trial roadmap** card is at the bottom of the side menu. It shows how many missions you've done and how many days are left.

<Passos>
  <Passo titulo={<>Click the <strong>Free trial roadmap</strong> card, at the bottom of the side menu.</>}>
    The list of missions opens.
  </Passo>
  <Passo titulo={<>Tap a mission's video to see how to do it.</>} />
  <Passo titulo={<>Click <strong>Go</strong> to open the mission's screen.</>}>
    Do the task on the platform. The mission is completed automatically.
  </Passo>
</Passos>

## The 6 missions

| # | Mission | Where to do it | Guide page |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Create your first course** | My Mentorships | [Create your first mentorship](/docs/cursos/mentorias/criar-mentoria) |
| 2 | **Add your first student** | My Clients | [Add a student](/docs/alunos/cadastrar-aluno) |
| 3 | **Follow your students' journey** | Tracking | [Tracking](/docs/alunos/acompanhar/acompanhamento) |
| 4 | **Connect your Google account** | Integrations | [Integrations](/docs/integracoes/intro) |
| 5 | **Schedule your first meeting** | My Meetings | [Create a meeting](/docs/grupos-e-equipe/reunioes/criar-reuniao) |
| 6 | **Create your first group** | Groups | [Create a group](/docs/grupos-e-equipe/grupos/criar-grupo) |

## Good to know

:::info How a mission is completed
You don't need to check anything. The mission is completed as soon as you do the task on the platform. Mission 3 is the easiest: just open the **Tracking** screen.
:::

:::tip
Want to keep going after the trial? In the roadmap, click **Subscribe before the free trial ends**.
:::

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>I don't see the roadmap in my menu. Why?</summary>

The roadmap only shows up for the account owner, while the free trial is active. People who signed in as team members don't see the roadmap.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I made the meeting in Google Calendar and the mission wasn't completed.</summary>

Meetings imported from Google Calendar don't count. Create the meeting inside Mentorfy, in **My Meetings**.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I hide the roadmap?</summary>

Yes, after you complete the 6 missions. Then the **Hide roadmap** button appears.

</details>
