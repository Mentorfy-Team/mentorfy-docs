The platform's terms, in alphabetical order.

:::tip The most important difference
**A mentorship is what you sell; a group is how you organize the people who bought it.**
:::

**Board**
The **Groups** screen, in columns. You can have several boards, one for each process or for each person on the team.

**Column**
Each stage of the **Groups** board, like "Onboarding" or "Completed". You move groups from one column to another as they move forward.

**Contact**
An event that shows the team talked to the group, like a comment on the card or a meeting. The **Indicators** in Groups count the days without contact to flag who needs attention. See [What counts as contact](/docs/alunos/acompanhar/o-que-conta-como-contato).

**Copilot (My Copilot)**
The AI that helps you run the platform. You ask in your own words, and it answers with your data, charts, and analyses.

**Credits**
The "currency" of the AI tools, like AI Studio and the Copilots. You see your balance in **My Account**. See [How much it costs](/docs/comece/quanto-custa).

**Group**
A set of students your team follows together: a class, or a company and its employees. Each group sets which products the students can access. Group tracking is internal: the student doesn't see it.

**Member area**
The space the student enters to watch the lessons. See [Member area](/docs/cursos/area-de-membros).

**Mentorship (or Product)**
What you sell: a course, a mentorship, or a member area. It is created in **My Mentorships**. "Mentorship" and "product" are the same item.

**Module and lesson**
A module groups the lessons of a mentorship. Each lesson is a **step** inside the module: video, text, file, task, and other types.

**Showcase**
The page your student sees, with their mentorships. The **Showcase Categories** are the shelves that make everything easy to find.

**Student (or Client)**
The person who buys and watches your mentorships. On the platform, students are in **My Clients**: "student" and "client" are the same person.

**Student Copilot**
An AI assistant for your students. It answers questions at any time, the way you teach it to.

**Team and team member**
In **My team**, you build your staff. A team brings together people who do the same work, like Customer Support. Each team member only accesses what you allow.

**Webhook and API**
These are ways to connect Mentorfy to other systems so they work on their own. Is there an API? Yes. In **Integrations**, on the **API Key** tab, you create a key. With it, another system can, for example, add students, create products, and give or remove access. Webhooks tell another system when something happens, or receive notices from a sales platform to give access automatically. See [Integrations](/docs/integracoes/intro).
