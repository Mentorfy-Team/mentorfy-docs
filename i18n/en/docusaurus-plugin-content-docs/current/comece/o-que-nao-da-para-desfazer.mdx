---

Almost everything in Mentorfy can be changed later. But some actions are **permanent**. Read this list before you delete something.

:::tip Golden rule
If the confirmation window says **"cannot be undone"** or **"irreversible"**, stop and check twice.
:::

## Students

- **Permanent:** **Remove Client** in **My Clients** deletes the student and their whole history.
  **Careful:** if you only want to take away access, use **Remove access** instead of removing the student.
- **Permanent:** **Merge duplicate accounts** deletes the account that was not kept. The products move to the other one.
  **Careful:** check which account will stay before you confirm.
- **Permanent:** **Remove access** with **Remove from the student's showcase** turned on deletes the student's link to the product, including the class.
  **Careful:** with that option turned off, the product is only locked, and you can give access again whenever you want.
- **Permanent:** **Reset answers** for a step, in the student report, deletes the current answer and the earlier versions of that step.
- **Permanent:** **Conclude Manually** a step for a student.

## Mentorships

- **Permanent:** **Deleting a mentorship** removes the mentorship, and students lose access to it.
  **Careful:** if you only want to hide it, hide the mentorship in the showcase instead.
- **Can be recovered:** **Deleted modules and steps** stay for **30 days** in **Restore Changes**, in the mentorship editor. When you restore them, the students' answers come back too.
- **Permanent:** deleting a question from the **Question Bank**.

## Groups

- **Permanent:** **Delete group** deletes the group for good. Students keep their access to the products.
- **Permanent:** deleting a group comment also deletes its replies.
- **No loss:** **Delete column** doesn't delete any group: they just move to another place on the board.
- **Can be recovered:** **Archive this board** takes the board off the list, but you can restore it later. No group is deleted.
- **Can be recovered:** when an automation moves a group to another column because the lesson started, the notice has an **Undo** button.

## Team

- **Can be recovered:** **Remove from team** takes the person out of that team. If they are not on another team, they lose access to your dashboard. To bring them back, invite them again with **Invite person**.
- **Permanent:** **Delete team**: the people on that team stop seeing the products that came from it and stop following the students there.

## Storage

- **Can be recovered:** **Delete** on a video, file, or folder sends the item to the **Trash**. It stays there for **30 days** and can be restored. Until then, students keep watching the video in the lessons.
- **Permanent:** **Empty trash** or deleting an item from inside it is permanent.
  **Careful:** items in the trash still use your plan's space. Empty it only when you are sure.

## Other

- **Permanent:** **Delete meeting** in **My Meetings**: the links and invites for that room stop working.
- **Permanent:** deleting an automation or a webhook in **Integrations**.
- **Permanent:** deleting an item from the **AI Studio** gallery.
- **Permanent:** deleting a comment in **Comments**: its replies disappear too.

## Common questions

<details>
<summary>I deleted something that couldn't be undone. Is there a way back?</summary>

Not through the platform. If it was a serious mistake, [contact support](/docs/conta/suporte) as soon as possible and explain what was deleted and when.
